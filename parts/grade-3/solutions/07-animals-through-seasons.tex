\section*{Chapter \ref{ch:g3:animals-through-seasons} --- Animals Through the Seasons}

\begin{solution}{exo:g3:animals-through-seasons:1}
Cold, which makes keeping warm cost more \omterm{prop:g3:balanced-diet:jobs}{energy} --- at the very
time when food, the source of that \omterm{prop:g3:balanced-diet:jobs}{energy}, becomes scarce.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g3:animals-through-seasons:2}
\omterm{def:g3:animals-through-seasons:migration}{Migrate}: the swallow, the stork (or crane). \omterm{def:g3:animals-through-seasons:hibernation}{Hibernate}: the
hedgehog, the marmot (or dormouse, bat). \omterm{def:g3:animals-through-seasons:active}{Stay active}: the fox,
the squirrel (or robin, deer).
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g3:animals-through-seasons:3}
Its body cools, its heart and breathing slow to almost nothing,
and it \omterm{prop:g1:healthy-habits:needs}{sleeps} deeply in its shelter. It lives off the fat it
stored up in autumn.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g3:animals-through-seasons:4}
The swallow eats flying \omterm{prop:g3:sorting-living-things:groups}{insects} caught on the wing --- and in
winter here there are none at all. With its food supply gone
completely, it must go where \omterm{prop:g3:sorting-living-things:groups}{insects} still fly: warmer lands.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g3:animals-through-seasons:5}
A thicker \omterm{def:g3:animals-through-seasons:active}{winter coat} (the fox, the deer); \omterm{def:g3:animals-through-seasons:active}{food stores} gathered
in autumn (the squirrel's buried nuts); a changed menu (the
blackbird switching from worms to berries); and harder
searching (the robin patrolling for the last berries).
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g3:animals-through-seasons:6}
The migrator risks an exhausting journey; the hibernator must
gather all its winter fat in autumn or not wake in spring; the
active \omterm{def:g1:animals-around-us:animal}{animal} gambles on finding food through the hungriest
months.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g3:animals-through-seasons:7}
Hibernation --- eating hugely builds the winter fat, the
leaf-lined hole is the winter nest. Step 4: the autumn check
(fattening up and digging a nest points to hibernation).
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g3:animals-through-seasons:8}
Open answer. No swallows in winter --- they are in Africa. The
\omterm{prop:g3:sorting-living-things:groups}{birds} actually seen (robins, tits, blackbirds, sparrows, crows)
are the active-strategy ones.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g3:animals-through-seasons:9}
(a) The squirrel's food is findable in winter --- especially
buried in autumn --- so: active, living on its stores. (b) The
crane's frogs and \omterm{prop:g3:sorting-living-things:groups}{insects} vanish from frozen wet meadows ---
food gone, so: \omterm{def:g3:animals-through-seasons:migration}{migration} (cranes indeed fly south in great
V-shaped flocks).
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g3:animals-through-seasons:10}
A hibernator pays for winter in advance, with autumn fat; a
thin hedgehog has nothing to burn through its months of cold
\omterm{prop:g1:healthy-habits:needs}{sleep} and will not wake. Rescuers take in too-thin autumn
hedgehogs and feed them indoors until they weigh enough to
\omterm{def:g3:animals-through-seasons:hibernation}{hibernate} safely --- or keep them warm and fed through the
winter.
\end{solution}
