\section*{Chapter \ref{ch:g8:reproductive-systems} --- The Reproductive Systems}

\begin{solution}{exo:g8:reproductive-systems:1}
The \omterm{def:g8:reproductive-systems:male}{testes}, in the scrotum: continuous sperm production
from \omterm{def:g8:puberty:puberty}{puberty}. The ducts and glands: storage, transport, and
the fluids that make semen. The \omterm{def:g8:reproductive-systems:male}{penis}: semen's exit --- and
\omterm{def:g7:removing-wastes:kidneys}{urine}'s, on its separate, valve-guarded duty.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g8:reproductive-systems:2}
The \omterm{def:g8:reproductive-systems:female}{ovaries}: the \omterm{def:g5:first-look-at-cells:cell}{egg-cell} stock from \omterm{def:g2:born-grow-die:cycle}{birth}, releasing one
per cycle from \omterm{def:g8:puberty:puberty}{puberty}. The \omterm{def:g8:reproductive-systems:female}{oviducts}: catching the released
\omterm{def:g2:animals-grow-up:born}{egg} --- the usual site of \omterm{def:g5:flower-to-fruit:steps}{fertilization}. The uterus: the
muscular, lining-renewed lodging. The vagina: reception of
semen, and the \omterm{def:g2:born-grow-die:cycle}{birth} canal.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g8:reproductive-systems:3}
\omterm{def:g8:reproductive-systems:male}{Sperm cell}: the body's smallest --- a nucleus-packed head
and a swimming tail, provisioned lightly, made continuously
by the hundreds of millions. \omterm{def:g4:animal-reproduction:sexual}{Egg cell}: the body's largest,
round and unmoving, richly provisioned, released one a
month.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g8:reproductive-systems:4}
An \omterm{def:g8:reproductive-systems:female}{ovary} releases one \omterm{def:g4:animal-reproduction:sexual}{egg cell}, caught by its \omterm{def:g8:reproductive-systems:female}{oviduct} ---
around day 14. It can be fertilized for about a day.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g8:reproductive-systems:5}
The breakdown and shedding of the uterus's prepared lining,
leaving over a few days. Its arrival says that cycle ended
without \omterm{def:g5:flower-to-fruit:steps}{fertilization}.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g8:reproductive-systems:6}
\omterm{def:g5:flower-to-fruit:steps}{Fertilization}. The \omterm{prop:g8:sexual-reproduction:core}{zygote} settles into the prepared lining,
the cycle suspends, and \omterm{def:g5:human-reproduction:pregnancy}{pregnancy} begins.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g8:reproductive-systems:7}
Step 1, \omterm{def:g8:sexual-reproduction:gametes}{gametes}: \omterm{def:g8:reproductive-systems:male}{testes} and \omterm{def:g8:reproductive-systems:female}{ovaries}. Step 2, \omterm{def:g5:flower-to-fruit:steps}{fertilization}:
in the \omterm{def:g8:reproductive-systems:female}{oviduct}, reached by the sperm's swim from vagina
through uterus. Step 3, the dividing \omterm{prop:g8:sexual-reproduction:core}{zygote}'s development:
lodged in the uterus's prepared lining.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g8:reproductive-systems:8}
The \omterm{def:g4:animal-reproduction:sexual}{male} side runs the many-and-cheap logic: continuous
swimming millions, each expendable. The \omterm{def:g4:animal-reproduction:sexual}{female} side runs
few-and-provisioned: one stocked \omterm{def:g4:animal-reproduction:sexual}{egg cell} a month, and
behind it the uterus's whole lodging investment. The
strategy line, written in \omterm{def:g5:first-look-at-cells:cell}{cells}.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g8:reproductive-systems:9}
The machinery is tuning in its first years --- the \omterm{def:g8:puberty:hormones}{hormone}
chain settling into rhythm --- so irregularity is expected;
and cycle length genuinely varies, person to person and
month to month. Both are the system running normally.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g8:reproductive-systems:10}
The uterus is a \omterm{def:g3:bones-and-muscles:muscle}{muscle}, and shedding is muscular work ---
its contractions are felt as cramps. Warmth and movement
help (and the school nurse has advice when they are not
enough).
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g8:reproductive-systems:11}
The sperm's job is the journey: cheap, swimming, and
needed in millions because almost none arrive --- so the
factory runs continuously. The \omterm{def:g2:animals-grow-up:born}{egg}'s job is to become the
\omterm{prop:g8:sexual-reproduction:core}{zygote}'s first provisions: costly, stocked from \omterm{def:g2:born-grow-die:cycle}{birth}, and
released with a month's preparation behind it --- so the
factory is a treasury, spending one at a time.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g8:reproductive-systems:12}
Event 1 is the question being prepared: the lining
rebuilt, the lodging readied. Event 2 poses it: one \omterm{def:g4:animal-reproduction:sexual}{egg
cell} released --- will \omterm{def:g5:flower-to-fruit:steps}{fertilization} come? Event 3 is the
answer ``no'': the unneeded lining sheds, and the wheel
asks again next month. Event 4 is the answer ``yes'': the
\omterm{prop:g8:sexual-reproduction:core}{zygote} settles, the asking stops, and the lodging serves
its purpose for nine months.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{pb:g8:reproductive-systems:1}
\textbf{1.} From the \omterm{def:g8:reproductive-systems:male}{testes} --- the factories, kept in the
scrotum --- sperm pass into the ducts for storage and
transport, are joined by the glands' fluids to make semen,
and leave through the \omterm{def:g8:reproductive-systems:male}{penis}; the same organ carries \omterm{def:g7:removing-wastes:kidneys}{urine}
at other times, a valve arrangement keeping the two duties
strictly apart.

\textbf{2.} The stock waits in the \omterm{def:g8:reproductive-systems:female}{ovaries}, from \omterm{def:g2:born-grow-die:cycle}{birth};
\omterm{def:g5:flower-to-fruit:steps}{fertilization} usually happens in the \omterm{def:g8:reproductive-systems:female}{oviducts}; a \omterm{def:g5:human-reproduction:pregnancy}{pregnancy}
lodges in the uterus's prepared lining.

\textbf{3.} The \omterm{def:g8:sexual-reproduction:gametes}{gametes} are built to opposite strategies:
millions of cheap swimmers against one provisioned \omterm{def:g2:animals-grow-up:born}{egg} a
month --- numbers-against-care, written into the \omterm{def:g5:first-look-at-cells:cell}{cells}
themselves.

\textbf{4.} Waiting for their orders: the systems stood
built but idle until the brain-base control center's
\omterm{def:g8:puberty:hormones}{hormones} switched them on at \omterm{def:g8:puberty:puberty}{puberty}.

\textbf{5.} Days 1--5 or so: the period. Through mid-cycle:
the lining rebuilds. Around day 14: \omterm{prop:g8:reproductive-systems:cycle}{ovulation}, with the
fertile day around it. Then the prepared wait --- to day
28 and the wheel's next turn.

\textbf{6.} Cycle length varies from person to person, and
month to month in one person --- and the first years run
irregular while the machinery tunes. The calendar teaches
the pattern, not anyone's exact dates.

\textbf{7.} Cancelled: the lining's breakdown and the next
period --- the wheel stops. Beginning: the \omterm{prop:g8:sexual-reproduction:core}{zygote}'s
settling into the lining, and \omterm{def:g5:human-reproduction:pregnancy}{pregnancy}'s nine months.

\textbf{8.} Cramps are the muscular uterus working as it
sheds --- normal, not damage; warmth and movement help,
and anything stronger has its address at the nurse's or
doctor's door.

\textbf{9.} For example: ``These are organs like \omterm{def:g4:heart-and-blood:heart}{heart} and
\omterm{def:g7:how-animals-breathe:four}{lungs} --- everyone in this room owns one set or the other,
and this is simply their chapter.''

\textbf{10.} Factories: \omterm{def:g8:reproductive-systems:male}{testes} --- \omterm{def:g8:reproductive-systems:female}{ovaries}. \omterm{def:g8:sexual-reproduction:gametes}{Gametes}:
continuous swimming millions --- one provisioned \omterm{def:g2:animals-grow-up:born}{egg} a
month. Meeting: the \omterm{def:g8:reproductive-systems:female}{oviduct}. Lodging: none --- the
monthly-prepared uterus. Timetable: continuous from
\omterm{def:g8:puberty:puberty}{puberty} --- cyclic, \omterm{def:g8:puberty:puberty}{puberty} to about fifty.

\textbf{11.} Step 1 on the two models: the factories make
the \omterm{def:g8:sexual-reproduction:gametes}{gametes}. Step 2 on the \omterm{def:g4:animal-reproduction:sexual}{female} model: their joining in
the \omterm{def:g8:reproductive-systems:female}{oviduct}. Step 3 across model and calendar: the \omterm{prop:g8:sexual-reproduction:core}{zygote}
lodging in the calendar's prepared lining, the wheel
stopped, development begun.

\textbf{12.} ``Because bodies run better on knowledge than
on legend --- and everything taught today has a trained,
confidential address for its follow-up questions.''
\end{solution}
