\begin{solution}{exo:g2:what-animals-eat:3}
Flat grinders --- \omterm{def:g1:plants-around-us:plant}{plants} like grass are tough and must be ground
for a long time before swallowing; there is no prey to seize.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g2:what-animals-eat:4}
Short and thick: cracking \omterm{def:g2:from-seed-to-plant:seed}{seeds}. Curved and hooked: tearing meat.
Long and thin: picking insects out of bark and crevices.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g2:what-animals-eat:5}
Tiny \omterm{def:g1:plants-around-us:plant}{plant} lice (aphids) --- the ladybug eats great numbers of
them, which makes gardeners love it.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g2:what-animals-eat:6}
Grass gives little nourishment at each mouthful, so the cow must
graze for hours to get enough; meat is rich, so one good meal can
last the fox a long while.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g2:what-animals-eat:7}
Worms and insects in summer; berries in winter, when the ground
is too hard for worms. A wide menu lets an \omterm{def:g2:what-animals-eat:kinds}{omnivore} switch foods
when one runs out --- a great help in hard seasons.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g2:what-animals-eat:8}
Open answer. Typically: sparrows and finches (short thick beaks)
take \omterm{def:g2:from-seed-to-plant:seed}{seeds}; tits pick insects and \omterm{def:g2:from-seed-to-plant:seed}{seeds}; blackbirds probe for
worms and take berries. The beak--menu match of the proposition
holds well.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g2:what-animals-eat:9}
A \omterm{def:g2:what-animals-eat:kinds}{carnivore}'s menu: other \omterm{def:g1:animals-around-us:animal}{animals}. Steps 1 and 2 --- the teeth
(pointed seizers) and the hunting tools (claws, forward \omterm{def:g1:the-five-senses:sense}{eyes});
step 4 named it.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g2:what-animals-eat:10}
The fox eats rabbits, and rabbits eat grass. No grass, no
rabbits; no rabbits, hungry foxes. The fox depends on grass
\emph{through} its prey --- meals in nature form chains.
\end{solution}
