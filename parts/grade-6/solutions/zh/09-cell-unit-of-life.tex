\begin{solution}{exo:g6:cell-unit-of-life:1}

\begin{solution}{exo:g6:cell-unit-of-life:4}

\begin{solution}{exo:g6:cell-unit-of-life:7}

\begin{solution}{exo:g6:cell-unit-of-life:12}
你身上的每个\omterm{def:g5:first-look-at-cells:cell}{细胞}都来自一次分裂，其母\omterm{def:g5:first-look-at-cells:cell}{细胞}

\omterm{def:g5:flower-to-fruit:steps}{受精}卵，再经父母的\omterm{def:g5:first-look-at-cells:cell}{细胞}，延伸至生命的

分裂链条上的最新一环，此链与生命

\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{pb:g6:cell-unit-of-life:1}
