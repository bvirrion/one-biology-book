purpose --- one restoring a failed correspondence, one
overriding a healthy one by choice.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g8:hormonal-communication:12}

channel, so broadcast becomes private lines. The push: a
\omterm{def:g5:first-look-at-cells:cell}{cell} joins an audience by building the receptor --- so

\omterm{def:g1:the-five-senses:sense}{skin}, growth plates --- were fitted with their receivers
in advance, waiting years for the first letter.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{pb:g8:hormonal-communication:1}
\textbf{1.} The pancreas's paired letters --- storage
\omterm{def:g8:hormonal-communication:glands}{hormone} on the rise, its releasing partner on the fall
--- holding the lane by \omterm{prop:g8:hormonal-communication:feedback}{feedback} while the owner slept.

\textbf{2.} Trigger: the meal's \omterm{prop:g7:organs-at-work:bill}{glucose} crossing the
\omterm{prop:g7:digestion-and-nutrients:absorption}{villi} and lifting the \omterm{prop:g4:journey-of-food:absorb}{blood} level. Writer: the pancreas.
Letter: the storage \omterm{def:g8:hormonal-communication:glands}{hormone}. Receivers: \omterm{prop:g7:digestion-and-nutrients:absorption}{liver} and
\omterm{def:g3:bones-and-muscles:muscle}{muscles}, banking the surplus.

\textbf{3.} A tap would pour past the goal into a
plunge; the descent leveling into the lane shows the
secretion subsiding as its own result arrives ---
self-measuring, self-stopping.

\textbf{4.} That it rose with the level and died back
with it: a curve of secretion shadowing the curve of
\omterm{prop:g7:organs-at-work:bill}{glucose}, peak trailing peak --- the thermostat's course.

\textbf{5.} The releasing partner (with the alarm
\omterm{def:g8:hormonal-communication:glands}{hormone}'s help in hard effort): the \omterm{prop:g7:digestion-and-nutrients:absorption}{liver}'s banked
\omterm{prop:g7:organs-at-work:bill}{glucose} --- the bank filled at breakfast in
\cref{ex:g7:digestion-and-nutrients:breadtrace}'s
manner --- spent into the working \omterm{def:g3:bones-and-muscles:muscle}{muscles}' bill.

\textbf{6.} The same releasing partner: steadily
unlocking the bank through the foodless hours, matching
the body's spending --- the lane held from the other
side.

\textbf{7.} The adrenal \omterm{def:g8:hormonal-communication:glands}{glands}. The sugar readies fuel
for \emph{flight or fight}: the alarm letter's whole
point is a body stocked and rerouted for immediate
effort --- whether or not the effort comes.

\textbf{8.} Because the healthy correspondence answered
it: the released surplus, unspent (the near-miss passed),
triggered the storage letter in its turn, and the lane
was restored --- alarm and thermostat in sequence.

\textbf{9.} Untreated: breakfast's rise climbing on,
undescending --- no storage letter written or heard ---
a high plateau through the morning; past the reclaiming
threshold, sugar appears in the \omterm{def:g7:removing-wastes:kidneys}{urine}
(\cref{ex:g7:removing-wastes:thrift}'s signal).

\textbf{10.} The pancreas's own mealtime letter. The
dose must match the plate because the letter's size must
match the \omterm{prop:g7:organs-at-work:bill}{glucose} it is to bank --- too small \omterm{def:g1:plants-around-us:parts}{leaves} a
pile-up, too large overshoots the lane downward, which
is its own emergency.

\textbf{11.} Overnight and afternoon: pancreas's pair,
\omterm{prop:g7:digestion-and-nutrients:absorption}{liver} and \omterm{def:g3:bones-and-muscles:muscle}{muscles} answering --- the lane held. Morning
and evening meals: \omterm{prop:g7:organs-at-work:bill}{glucose} speaks, pancreas writes,
\omterm{prop:g7:digestion-and-nutrients:absorption}{liver} banks. Noon: effort spends, the releasing partner
unlocks. Five o'clock: adrenals interject, pancreas
tidies after. One value, five speakers, no silence all
day.

\textbf{12.} ``A \omterm{prop:g8:hormonal-communication:feedback}{feedback} system: writers that read the
results of their own letters --- which is how a value
rides a narrow lane for a lifetime with nobody
driving.''
\end{solution}
