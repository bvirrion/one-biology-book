\begin{solution}{exo:g10:scientific-biology:2}
\emph{1.} Not testable as stated (``intelligence'' undefined). Rewrite:
``In a maze of fixed design, mean completion time of adult house cats is
shorter than that of adult dogs of comparable age and training history.''
\emph{2.} Testable as written.
\emph{3.} Not scientific (value judgement, not empirically decidable).
Rewrite only if recast into a measurable claim, e.g.\ ``In a blind survey,
\qty{80}{\percent} of respondents rate photograph set A higher on a 1--10
beauty scale than set B'' --- still not about ``life'' itself.
\emph{4.} Testable as written.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g10:scientific-biology:3}
Independent: caffeine concentration (e.g.\ \unit{mg/L} in the medium).
Dependent: heart rate (beats per minute). Controlled examples: species
and age of fleas, temperature, light, volume of medium, \omterm{def:g10:scientific-biology:observation}{observation} time

\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g10:scientific-biology:4}
Without a control, \omterm{def:g10:scientific-biology:properties}{growth} could be due to weather, water, soil or chance.
Control: same tomato variety, soil, water, light and spacing, with no
fertiliser (or with a standard fertiliser if the claim is ``better than
current products''). Compare yields in \unit{kg} per plant or per plot.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g10:scientific-biology:5}
Any five with plant examples, e.g.: organisation (cells, leaves);
\omterm{def:g10:scientific-biology:properties}{metabolism} (photosynthesis, respiration); \omterm{def:g10:scientific-biology:properties}{homeostasis} (stomata regulate
water loss); \omterm{def:g10:scientific-biology:properties}{growth} (new leaves); \omterm{def:g10:scientific-biology:properties}{reproduction} (flowers/seeds); response
(leaves turn toward light); adaptation (drought-tolerant varieties over
generations of selection).
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g10:scientific-biology:6}
Weaknesses and fixes (sample):
\begin{enumerate}
  \item $n=1$ plant each --- use many replicates per room.
  \item Rooms differ in light, temperature, drafts --- same chamber,
        only sound differs, or swap positions.
  \item No true control of ``music type/volume'' vs silence with matched
        vibration --- measure sound and vibration.
  \item Height alone may reflect etiolation --- measure dry mass too.
  \item Observer bias --- blind measurement.
  \item No \omterm{def:g10:scientific-biology:hypothesis}{hypothesis} stated in advance --- pre-register prediction.
\end{enumerate}
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g10:scientific-biology:7}
Horizontal: daily light duration (\unit{h}); vertical: mean leaf area
(\unit{cm^2}) with error bars if available. Control at \qty{0}{h} or
minimal natural light as defined. Support for light-limited \omterm{def:g10:scientific-biology:properties}{growth} with

\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g10:scientific-biology:8}
For: genetic material, evolution under selection, structure of proteins
and nucleic acids. Against: no independent \omterm{def:g10:scientific-biology:properties}{metabolism}, no \omterm{def:g10:scientific-biology:properties}{growth} by
cellular machinery, \omterm{def:g10:scientific-biology:properties}{reproduction} only via host. Working conclusion for
this course: viruses are acellular obligate parasites, not free-living
\omterm{def:g10:scientific-biology:organism}{organisms}; \omterm{prop:g10:scientific-biology:cell-unit}{cell theory} still holds for free-living life.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g10:scientific-biology:9}
\omterm{def:g10:scientific-biology:hypothesis}{Hypothesis}: provisional testable claim. \omterm{def:g10:scientific-biology:theory}{Theory}: broad, well-tested
explanatory framework. \omterm{def:g10:scientific-biology:theory}{Law}: often a compact regular pattern (less central
in \omterm{def:g10:scientific-biology:science}{biology}). \omterm{prop:g10:scientific-biology:cell-unit}{Cell theory} is a \omterm{def:g10:scientific-biology:theory}{theory}; ``all \omterm{def:g10:scientific-biology:organism}{organisms} are made of cells''
is one of its core generalisations, continually tested against exceptions
(e.g.\ acellular viruses, which are not free-living \omterm{def:g10:scientific-biology:organism}{organisms}).
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g10:scientific-biology:10}
\omterm{def:g10:scientific-biology:hypothesis}{Hypothesis} example: in a two-choice chamber, more worms are found in moist
soil after \qty{20}{min}. Independent: moisture level; dependent: count
of worms per side. Controlled: temperature, light, soil type, worm age.
Many worms, randomised start positions. Support: significant preference
for moist side; reject: no difference or preference for dry.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g10:scientific-biology:11}
Questions: exact sample size and power; variability and effect size, not
only ``significant''; replication; blinding and randomisation; dose and
controls; whether the memory test is validated; conflicts of interest;
whether the result has been reproduced independently.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{pb:g10:scientific-biology:1}
\textbf{Part I.}
\emph{1.} Example: ``Is mean percentage lichen cover on tree trunks of
species $X$ lower within \qty{50}{m} of road R than in park P?''
\emph{2.} \omterm{def:g10:scientific-biology:hypothesis}{Hypothesis}: mean cover is lower near the road if pollution
reduces lichen \omterm{def:g10:scientific-biology:properties}{growth}; prediction: road mean $<$ park mean under matched
sampling.
\emph{3.} Independent: site type (road/park); dependent: lichen cover
(\% of quadrat or Braun--Blanquet score). Matched variables: tree
species, trunk diameter class, sample height, aspect (N/S), recent rain,
season, bark type.

\textbf{Part II.}
\emph{1.} Random or systematic selection of $\ge 10$ trees per site;
quadrat at fixed height (e.g.\ \qty{1.5}{m}) on fixed aspect; several
quadrats per tree.
\emph{2.} One tree confounds individual history with site; no estimate of
variation.
\emph{3.} Traffic safety; do not damage trees or remove protected
species; permission for private land.

\textbf{Part III.}
\emph{1.} Humidity, tree age, light, bark pH, herbivory, different species
mix, recent pruning.
\emph{2.} Measure humidity, light, bark pH, diameter, pollution proxies
(e.g.\ NO$_2$ badges), identify lichen species.
\emph{3.} Sites are not assigned by the investigator; many variables
covary with traffic. Association is not proof of causation; a controlled
fumigation \omterm{def:g10:scientific-biology:experiment}{experiment} would be needed for a causal claim about a named
pollutant.
\end{solution}
