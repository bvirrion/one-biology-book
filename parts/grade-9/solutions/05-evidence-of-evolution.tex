\section*{Chapter \ref{ch:g9:evidence-of-evolution} --- Evidence of Evolution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g9:evidence-of-evolution:1}
A preserved trace of past life sealed in sedimentary rock.
Layers stack in deposition order, oldest lowest --- so
upward through the stack is forward through time.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g9:evidence-of-evolution:2}
Faunas change layer by layer, most deep \omterm{def:g5:biodiversity:species}{species} \omterm{prop:g5:biodiversity:loss}{extinct};
the great groups appear in the tree's nested order ---
\omterm{prop:g3:sorting-living-things:groups}{fish} deep, four-limbed kin above, \omterm{prop:g3:sorting-living-things:groups}{mammals} and \omterm{prop:g3:sorting-living-things:groups}{birds} high;
and intermediates stand at the \omterm{def:g1:plants-around-us:tree}{branch} points.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g9:evidence-of-evolution:3}
\omterm{def:g1:animals-around-us:covering}{Feathers} and wings --- the bird kit --- on a frame with
teeth and a long bony tail --- the reptile kit: both decks
in one \omterm{def:g1:animals-around-us:animal}{animal}, at the fork the tree draws.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g9:evidence-of-evolution:4}
The whale's hidden hip-and-leg \omterm{def:g3:bones-and-muscles:skeleton}{bones}: a walking ancestry.
The flightless beetle's sealed wings: flying ancestors.
The human tailbone (or shrunken appendix): a tailed,
differently-fed line. Each is a kept part testifying to a
former build.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g9:evidence-of-evolution:5}
Count a shared \omterm{def:g9:genes-and-dna:gene}{gene}'s letter-differences between \omterm{def:g5:biodiversity:species}{species}:
the fewer, the closer the kin. The distances, compiled,
assemble a tree --- and it matches the one \omterm{def:g3:bones-and-muscles:skeleton}{bones} and
\omterm{def:g9:evidence-of-evolution:fossils}{fossils} drew.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g9:evidence-of-evolution:6}
The peppered moth's dark form tracking soot and clean air;
insecticide- and antibiotic-proof pests arising in years;
the finches' beak measurements shifting with drought and
plenty.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g9:evidence-of-evolution:7}
Fresh engineering could give each \omterm{def:g1:my-body:parts}{limb} its best mechanism
--- struts for wings, hydrofoils for flippers --- and
would repeat no arbitrary \omterm{def:g3:bones-and-muscles:skeleton}{bone} count. The one arrangement,
awkwardly stretched to every trade, is what building-on-
inheritance produces and free design would not.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g9:evidence-of-evolution:8}
Class: \omterm{def:g2:animals-grow-up:born}{milk}, \omterm{def:g7:what-is-respiration:respiration}{air-breathing}, fetal hair --- mammal
heirlooms (\cref{ex:g6:classification-kinship:whale}).
Vestiges: hidden hips and \omterm{def:g1:my-body:parts}{leg} \omterm{def:g3:bones-and-muscles:skeleton}{bones}. \omterm{def:g9:evidence-of-evolution:fossils}{Fossils}: the
time-ordered series from four-legged shore mammal to
legless swimmer. Molecules: its gene-distances place it
among the \omterm{prop:g3:sorting-living-things:groups}{mammals}, hoofed kin nearest. Four witnesses, one
dossier.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g9:evidence-of-evolution:9}
Because the witnesses could not collude: rocks were laid,
bodies built and molecules spelled by processes with no
access to each other's testimony. Independent witnesses
agreeing multiply credibility --- one tree from three
sources is the convergence the method prizes.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g9:evidence-of-evolution:10}
Because the claim forbids it: \omterm{prop:g3:sorting-living-things:groups}{mammals} must not appear
below the layers of their ancestors. Every dig is a fresh
chance to falsify --- and the absence, sustained across
every excavation ever made, is the claim passing its test
daily.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g9:evidence-of-evolution:11}
\omterm{def:g4:classifying-animals:vertebrate}{Vertebrate} \omterm{def:g5:human-reproduction:pregnancy}{embryos} agree strikingly through the early
plan-laying weeks and diverge late. The development
chapter's machinery says why: early instructions lay the
shared body plan --- the deepest, oldest chapters of the
text --- while later reading writes the \omterm{def:g5:biodiversity:species}{species}'
particulars. Shared early text, shared deep ancestry.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g9:evidence-of-evolution:12}
Claim: \omterm{def:g5:biodiversity:species}{species} change and share descent. Predictions:
time-ordered faunas in the tree's nesting; intermediates
at forks; vestiges of former builds; molecular distances
grading with \omterm{prop:g6:classification-kinship:kinship}{kinship}. Witnesses: the rocks deliver the
order and the intermediates; bodies deliver plan and
vestiges; molecules deliver the matching tree; live
watches deliver change in motion. Verdict: descent with
change stands on convergent independent testimony ---
and hands the next chapter its question: \emph{what
drives the change?}
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{pb:g9:evidence-of-evolution:1}
\textbf{1.} Order: trilobites lowest, then first \omterm{prop:g3:sorting-living-things:groups}{fish},
amphibian-kin tracks, \omterm{prop:g4:classifying-animals:classes}{reptiles}, \emph{Archaeopteryx},
early mammal jaw. Caption: ``Read upward, the rocks
change cast in exactly the order the family tree
predicts --- with the in-betweens standing at the
branchings.''

\textbf{2.} Caption: ``One \omterm{def:g3:bones-and-muscles:skeleton}{bone} plan --- stretched to
fly, paddle, gallop and write: inheritance re-jobbed,
never redesigned.'' Cabinet: the whale's hips, the sealed
beetle wings, the human tailbone.

\textbf{3.} The tree implied by the letter-counts ---
chimp nearest, \omterm{ex:g6:producing-our-food:bread}{yeast} farthest --- must match the
anatomists' and paleontologists' tree next door: same
branching, independently derived.

\textbf{4.} The moths: a \omterm{def:g8:reproduction-and-environment:population}{population}'s colors tracking its
\omterm{def:g6:exploring-our-environment:components}{environment} within decades. The finches: beak
distributions moving with the \omterm{def:g2:from-seed-to-plant:seed}{seed} supply, caliper-measured
--- inheritance-fed change, watched live.

\textbf{5.} Because descent \omterm{def:g1:plants-around-us:tree}{branches}: we and today's
monkeys are cousins on separate \omterm{def:g1:plants-around-us:tree}{branches} of a shared
fork --- no one alive is anyone's ancestor, and a
cousin's existence no more denies \omterm{prop:g6:classification-kinship:kinship}{kinship} than your
cousins deny yours.

\textbf{6.} \emph{Archaeopteryx}, standing between
reptile frame and bird kit; the legged \omterm{def:g9:evidence-of-evolution:fossils}{fossil} whales,
standing between shore mammal and swimmer. Both were
predictions before they were finds.

\textbf{7.} In science ``theory'' names an explanation
tested until it organizes a field --- not a guess. What
the rooms establish is the historical fact of change and
descent, by convergent independent witnesses; the theory
proper is the mechanism the next hall explains.

\textbf{8.} A true anachronism --- mammal \omterm{def:g3:bones-and-muscles:skeleton}{bones} below
trilobite layers, verified --- would break the order the
claim demands. That no dig in two centuries has produced
one is the claim surviving its hardest test, every day,
everywhere digging continues.

\textbf{9.} ``The boxes-in-boxes you met next door were
never filing convenience: \omterm{def:g6:classification-kinship:attribute}{attributes} nest because
descent \omterm{def:g1:plants-around-us:tree}{branches} --- the \omterm{def:g4:classifying-animals:classification}{classification} was the family
tree, drawn before anyone knew what it drew.''

\textbf{10.} ``Every exhibit in this wing, and every
visitor before it, writes its build in the same four
letters read by the same code: the \omterm{def:g1:plants-around-us:tree}{branches} differ, the
trunk's language never has.''

\textbf{11.} ``This wing shows that \omterm{def:g5:biodiversity:species}{species} change, that
they share descent, and that the record keeps the
in-betweens. What it has not yet said is what \emph{drives}
the change --- the mechanism hall is next, and its answer
runs on variety, inheritance and the \omterm{def:g6:exploring-our-environment:components}{environment}'s
checks.''

\textbf{12.} ``All life is one family album, and the
rocks kept the early pages --- given time, cousins grow
as different as whales and bats, and stay family.''
\end{solution}
