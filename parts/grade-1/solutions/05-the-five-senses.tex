\section*{Chapter \ref{ch:g1:the-five-senses} --- The Five Senses}

\begin{solution}{exo:g1:the-five-senses:1}
Sight --- the \omterm{def:g1:the-five-senses:sense}{eyes}; hearing --- the \omterm{def:g1:the-five-senses:sense}{ears}; smell --- the nose;
taste --- the tongue; touch --- the \omterm{def:g1:the-five-senses:sense}{skin}.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g1:the-five-senses:2}
(a) The \omterm{def:g1:the-five-senses:sense}{eyes}. (b) The \omterm{def:g1:the-five-senses:sense}{skin}. (c) The nose. (d) The \omterm{def:g1:the-five-senses:sense}{ears}.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g1:the-five-senses:3}
The \omterm{def:g1:the-five-senses:sense}{skin}, the organ of touch. It covers the whole body, from head
to toe.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g1:the-five-senses:4}
The \omterm{def:g1:the-five-senses:sense}{skin} of the hands sent the message (cold, hard, jagged); the
brain understood it and named the object.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g1:the-five-senses:5}
Taste and smell. The nose does half the work of flavor, which is
why food seems dull when it is blocked.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g1:the-five-senses:6}
For example: never look straight at the Sun --- protects the \omterm{def:g1:the-five-senses:sense}{eyes};
keep sounds gentle --- protects the \omterm{def:g1:the-five-senses:sense}{ears}; blow on hot food ---
protects the tongue; nothing pushed into \omterm{def:g1:the-five-senses:sense}{ear} or nose --- protects
\omterm{def:g1:the-five-senses:sense}{ears} and nose; good light for reading --- protects the \omterm{def:g1:the-five-senses:sense}{eyes}.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g1:the-five-senses:7}
Because too-loud sound hurts the \omterm{def:g1:the-five-senses:sense}{ears}, and a hurt \omterm{def:g1:the-five-senses:sense}{ear} may stay
hurt. The covers keep the machines' noise gentle enough for a
lifetime of hearing.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g1:the-five-senses:8}
Open answer --- most people name two or three out of three. The
nose recognizes familiar foods well, even with no help from the
\omterm{def:g1:the-five-senses:sense}{eyes}.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g1:the-five-senses:9}
The \omterm{def:g1:the-five-senses:sense}{eye} only \emph{sees} --- it collects light and sends a message.
The brain does the understanding: it receives the message and says
``that is Grandma'', ``that is my school''. Senses collect; the
brain understands.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g1:the-five-senses:10}
Reading: with the fingertips --- touch takes over from sight,
feeling raised dots on the page. Crossing the street: with the
\omterm{def:g1:the-five-senses:sense}{ears} --- listening for cars coming and going --- often with a cane
whose tip lets touch check the ground ahead.
\end{solution}
