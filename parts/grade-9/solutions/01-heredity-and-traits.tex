\section*{Chapter \ref{ch:g9:heredity-and-traits} --- Heredity and Traits}

\begin{solution}{exo:g9:heredity-and-traits:1}
A \omterm{def:g9:heredity-and-traits:traits}{trait} is any describable characteristic of an individual;
\omterm{def:g9:heredity-and-traits:heredity}{heredity} is the transmission of \omterm{def:g9:heredity-and-traits:traits}{traits} from parents to
offspring through reproduction. The sorting test: what two
\omterm{def:g8:sexual-reproduction:gametes}{gametes} cannot carry, families cannot transmit.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g9:heredity-and-traits:2}
\omterm{prop:g4:journey-of-food:absorb}{Blood} group: \omterm{def:g9:heredity-and-traits:heredity}{hereditary}. Pierced \omterm{def:g1:the-five-senses:sense}{ear}: acquired. Freckles:
\omterm{def:g9:heredity-and-traits:heredity}{hereditary} tendency, sun-lived (both workings). Fluent
French: acquired. Earlobe shape: \omterm{def:g9:heredity-and-traits:heredity}{hereditary}.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g9:heredity-and-traits:3}
Family pattern (recurs along reproduction's lines, or
follows lives); presence from the start (fixed at
\omterm{def:g5:flower-to-fruit:steps}{fertilization}, or built after); transmission (passes to
children, or dies with its acquirer).
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g9:heredity-and-traits:4}
Because trained \omterm{def:g3:bones-and-muscles:muscle}{muscle} is acquired --- built by use, not
carried by \omterm{def:g8:sexual-reproduction:gametes}{gametes}. It shows that what a life adds to a
body is not written into what reproduction transmits.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g9:heredity-and-traits:5}
Transmit what they do not show: the \omterm{def:g9:heredity-and-traits:traits}{trait}'s determinant
traveled through them hidden, and surfaced a generation
on.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g9:heredity-and-traits:6}
Because \omterm{def:g9:heredity-and-traits:traits}{traits} themselves (a chin, a \omterm{prop:g4:journey-of-food:absorb}{blood} group) cannot
ride in two single \omterm{def:g5:first-look-at-cells:cell}{cells} --- what rides must be
instructions for building them.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g9:heredity-and-traits:7}
Inherited: the height range the frame suggests --- the
grandson received the family's determinants. Lived: richer
feeding filled the range further --- the \omterm{def:g6:exploring-our-environment:components}{environment}'s
addition on top of the inheritance.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g9:heredity-and-traits:8}
Each child receives a different selection of each parent's
determinants --- the shuffle deals fresh combinations per
\omterm{def:g5:flower-to-fruit:steps}{fertilization}. Identical twins split \emph{after} one
\omterm{def:g5:flower-to-fruit:steps}{fertilization}: one deal, two builds --- so their
determinant sets match.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g9:heredity-and-traits:9}
(a) Follows the tree, appears at its family age
regardless of trade or town, conceivably gamete-carried:
\omterm{def:g9:heredity-and-traits:heredity}{hereditary}. (b) Built by training, follows the sport not
the parents, no \omterm{def:g8:sexual-reproduction:gametes}{gamete} carries stamina: acquired --- on an
inherited range of build and \omterm{def:g4:heart-and-blood:heart}{heart}
(\cref{prop:g7:organs-at-work:training} builds what
\omterm{def:g9:heredity-and-traits:heredity}{heredity} only sketches).
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g9:heredity-and-traits:10}
Never the skill --- \omterm{def:g8:nervous-communication:synapse}{synapses} train in each life anew. What
may pass: aptitude's raw materials --- \omterm{def:g1:the-five-senses:sense}{ear}, dexterity,
temperament --- an inherited range that musical households
then fill with practice, tuition and example (the second,
living-together channel of transmission).
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g9:heredity-and-traits:11}
Skipping is \omterm{def:g9:heredity-and-traits:heredity}{heredity} behaving normally: each parent can
carry the \omterm{def:g1:the-five-senses:sense}{blue-eye} determinant unshown and hand it on; two
such hidden copies meeting in one child display the \omterm{def:g9:heredity-and-traits:traits}{trait}.
The pattern proves carriage-without-showing --- not
anything about the family's honesty.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g9:heredity-and-traits:12}
Everything the father transmits rides in the \omterm{def:g8:reproductive-systems:male}{sperm cell},
and the \omterm{def:g8:reproductive-systems:male}{sperm cell} is a tail and a packed \omterm{def:g6:cell-unit-of-life:parts}{nucleus} ---
so the determinants must sit in the \omterm{def:g6:cell-unit-of-life:parts}{nucleus}. And since a
whole human's paternal instructions fit in a package
thousandths of a millimetre across, the instructions must
be written at molecular smallness --- a script finer than
any \omterm{def:g5:first-look-at-cells:cell}{cell} structure the school \omterm{def:g5:first-look-at-cells:microscope}{microscope} shows.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{pb:g9:heredity-and-traits:1}
\textbf{1.} Chapel chin: \omterm{def:g9:heredity-and-traits:heredity}{hereditary} (family pattern,
present from the start). Left-handedness: \omterm{def:g9:heredity-and-traits:heredity}{hereditary}
tendency (follows families loosely, fixed early). Bakers'
cough: acquired (follows the trade). \omterm{prop:g4:journey-of-food:absorb}{Blood} groups:
\omterm{def:g9:heredity-and-traits:heredity}{hereditary} (fixed at \omterm{def:g5:flower-to-fruit:steps}{fertilization}, gamete-carriable).
Weathered faces: acquired (follow the shepherding life).

\textbf{2.} The family-pattern test: the \omterm{def:g9:heredity-and-traits:traits}{trait} maps onto
an occupation's lines, not reproduction's --- marrying in
acquires it, moving away loses it, no \omterm{def:g8:sexual-reproduction:gametes}{gamete} involved.

\textbf{3.} That a determinant can be carried without
being shown: the middle generation transmitted the chin's
instructions while displaying other chins.

\textbf{4.} Each parent carried, alongside its shown
group's determinant, a hidden group-O determinant --- and
the child received the two hidden ones. Carriage unshown,
in the register's own ink.

\textbf{5.} Not the chin but its determinants ---
instructions for building it --- traveling in the only
vehicles reproduction has: one \omterm{def:g4:animal-reproduction:sexual}{egg cell} and one \omterm{def:g8:reproductive-systems:male}{sperm
cell} per generation, four handovers in the century.

\textbf{6.} In the selections: each \omterm{def:g5:flower-to-fruit:steps}{fertilization} dealt
the shared chin determinant alongside a fresh half-and-half
selection of everything else --- common card, different
hands.

\textbf{7.} Acquired \omterm{def:g9:heredity-and-traits:traits}{traits} are never transmitted.
Reason: they are built in the body's tissues by living,
and the \omterm{def:g8:sexual-reproduction:gametes}{gametes} --- two \omterm{def:g5:first-look-at-cells:cell}{cells}, carrying determinants
only --- have no way to record them.

\textbf{8.} One \omterm{def:g5:flower-to-fruit:steps}{fertilization}, later split: a single
determinant deal built twice ---
\cref{rem:g5:human-reproduction:twins}'s identical twins,
certified by the ledger's matching.

\textbf{9.} \omterm{def:g8:sexual-reproduction:gametes}{Gamete} channel: chin, \omterm{prop:g4:journey-of-food:absorb}{blood} groups,
left-handedness's tendency. Living-together channel: the
cough (the bakery's air), the weathering (the hills'
weather) --- and, from the album chapter, languages,
trades and skills.

\textbf{10.} From the skips: the middle generation
carried the chin unshown. From the \omterm{prop:g4:journey-of-food:absorb}{blood} register: A- and
B-showing parents carried O unshown. Twice over, carriage
exceeds display.

\textbf{11.} Physically: they sit in the \omterm{def:g8:sexual-reproduction:gametes}{gametes}' nuclei
--- that much the sperm's anatomy already proves. What
they are made of, and how so small a package writes so
large a book, is precisely the next chapters' question.

\textbf{12.} Most \omterm{def:g9:heredity-and-traits:traits}{traits} are inherited ranges, lived
into: the \omterm{def:g8:sexual-reproduction:gametes}{gametes} deal the sketch, the life draws it in.
\end{solution}
