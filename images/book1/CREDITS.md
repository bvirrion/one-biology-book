The images under `images/book1/ai/` are AI-generated illustrations,
produced with OpenAI image generation driven through the Codex CLI
(`codex exec`), from editor-written prompts recorded in
`images/book1/ai/PROMPTS.md`, and visually reviewed for biological

on the book's Image Credits page (`frontmatter/image-credits.tex`).
