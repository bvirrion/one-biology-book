\section*{Chapter \ref{ch:g8:nervous-communication} --- Nervous Communication}

\begin{solution}{exo:g8:nervous-communication:1}
A \omterm{def:g8:nervous-communication:neuron}{neuron} is the \omterm{def:g5:brain-in-command:system}{nervous system}'s working \omterm{def:g5:first-look-at-cells:cell}{cell}: a \omterm{def:g5:first-look-at-cells:cell}{cell} body
with a long thin fiber --- some spine-to-toe. A \omterm{def:g8:nervous-communication:neuron}{nerve} is a
cable bundling thousands of such fibers.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g8:nervous-communication:2}
Brief electrical signals, at up to \qty{100}{m/s}. The
signals are all alike; intensity is coded as their rate ---
gentle patter to rapid drumroll.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g8:nervous-communication:3}
Sensory \omterm{def:g8:nervous-communication:neuron}{neurons}: the report lines, \omterm{def:g1:the-five-senses:sense}{sense organ} to cord and
\omterm{def:g5:brain-in-command:system}{brain}. Motor \omterm{def:g8:nervous-communication:neuron}{neurons}: the order lines, cord to \omterm{def:g3:bones-and-muscles:muscle}{muscle}.
Connecting \omterm{def:g8:nervous-communication:neuron}{neurons}: the deciding, within \omterm{def:g5:brain-in-command:system}{brain} and cord.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g8:nervous-communication:4}
The microscopic gap where one \omterm{def:g8:nervous-communication:neuron}{neuron}'s fiber end meets the
next \omterm{def:g5:first-look-at-cells:cell}{cell}. A chemical messenger crosses it, docks, and ---
if enough arrives --- restarts the signal in the next
\omterm{def:g8:nervous-communication:neuron}{neuron}.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g8:nervous-communication:5}
Adjustability (\omterm{def:g8:nervous-communication:synapse}{synapses} strengthen with use and fade
without --- learning); one-way traffic (release happens on
one side only); and weighing (each \omterm{def:g8:nervous-communication:neuron}{neuron} sums thousands
of exciting and damping inputs before firing).
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g8:nervous-communication:6}
\omterm{def:g5:brain-in-command:system}{Nerves}: milliseconds, one precise address, dedicated wire
paths, effect gone with the signal. \omterm{def:g8:puberty:hormones}{Hormones}: minutes to
years, body-wide, bloodstream broadcast, effect running as
long as the messenger circulates.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g8:nervous-communication:7}
The \omterm{def:g1:the-five-senses:sense}{eyes}' sensory \omterm{def:g8:nervous-communication:neuron}{neurons} drum the ball's positions to the
\omterm{def:g5:brain-in-command:system}{brain}; connecting \omterm{def:g8:nervous-communication:neuron}{neurons} weigh the streams at their
\omterm{def:g8:nervous-communication:synapse}{synapses} --- the path computed is inputs summed junction
by junction; motor \omterm{def:g8:nervous-communication:neuron}{neurons} drum the orders out, their
rates setting each \omterm{def:g3:bones-and-muscles:muscle}{muscle}'s strength for step, lean and
close of the hand.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g8:nervous-communication:8}
Each practiced pass conducts signals across the same
junctions, and crossed junctions strengthen: after a month
the juggle's routing is pre-weighted --- what needed
effortful weighing now runs of itself. The permanence is
stronger \omterm{def:g8:nervous-communication:synapse}{synapses}.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g8:nervous-communication:9}
Because the \omterm{def:g8:nervous-communication:synapse}{synapse} is where the signal becomes chemistry
--- a released messenger, docking sites, a gap open to
whatever molecules arrive. A wire's electricity is hard to
counterfeit; a chemical courier can be imitated, blocked
or exhausted --- so that is where anesthetics, venoms and
drugs all act.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g8:nervous-communication:10}
(a) Milliseconds, one \omterm{def:g1:my-body:parts}{arm}'s \omterm{def:g3:bones-and-muscles:muscle}{muscles}, wired path, gone at
once: nervous. (b) Years, body-wide, bloodstream, running
for seasons: hormonal. (c) The relay: a nervous
split-second start, then the bloodstream messenger holding
the alert for minutes after the scare --- both, in order.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g8:nervous-communication:11}
The order arrives at the junction and cannot cross: \omterm{def:g3:bones-and-muscles:muscle}{muscles}
receive nothing. The victim is progressively paralyzed ---
conscious, sensing, deciding --- with the loop cut at its
last link, order-to-muscle.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g8:nervous-communication:12}
Learning: skills are \omterm{def:g8:nervous-communication:synapse}{synapses} strengthened by use --- the
juggler's month lives at junctions. Deciding: each \omterm{def:g8:nervous-communication:neuron}{neuron}
fires or not by summing its junctions' voices --- the
loop's ``decide'' is junction arithmetic. Vulnerability:
anesthetics, venoms and drugs act where signal becomes
chemistry --- at the gap. Wires only carry; junctions
learn, choose and can be fooled.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{pb:g8:nervous-communication:1}
\textbf{1.} The tendon stretch fires sensory \omterm{def:g8:nervous-communication:neuron}{neurons}; they
run to the \emph{\omterm{def:g5:brain-in-command:system}{spinal cord}}, \omterm{def:g8:nervous-communication:synapse}{synapse} there directly onto
motor \omterm{def:g8:nervous-communication:neuron}{neurons}, and the motor drumroll kicks the thigh
\omterm{def:g3:bones-and-muscles:muscle}{muscle}. Two \omterm{def:g8:nervous-communication:neuron}{neuron} kinds, one junction, cord-level ---
the \omterm{def:g5:brain-in-command:system}{brain} is bypassed because protection cannot wait for
deliberation.

\textbf{2.} The catch's loop runs \omterm{def:g1:the-five-senses:sense}{eye} to \omterm{def:g5:brain-in-command:system}{brain} to \omterm{def:g1:my-body:parts}{arm} ---
metres of fiber and many weighing junctions; the knee-jerk
runs tendon to cord to \omterm{def:g3:bones-and-muscles:muscle}{muscle} --- centimetres and
essentially one \omterm{def:g8:nervous-communication:synapse}{synapse}. Shorter wire, fewer junctions,
a tenth the time.

\textbf{3.} The decision is made in the cord, before any
\omterm{def:g5:brain-in-command:system}{brain} veto can arrive --- and the \omterm{def:g8:nervous-communication:synapse}{synapses}' one-way
traffic means the \omterm{def:g5:brain-in-command:system}{brain}'s later objection cannot run
backward down the sensory line. Warning changes nothing:
the loop closes below the will.

\textbf{4.} The feeling is a second journey: the same
tap's report also climbs the cord to the \omterm{def:g5:brain-in-command:system}{brain} --- a
longer path with more junctions --- arriving after the
kick is already history.

\textbf{5.} Frequency coding: the harder stretch sends a
faster drumroll, and the motor line answers in kind ---
stronger contraction from a higher rate, the one-word
language at both ends.

\textbf{6.} That the whole circuit is sound: sensory line,
cord junction, motor line and \omterm{def:g3:bones-and-muscles:muscle}{muscle} --- and symmetry
certifies both sides alike. One tap audits four
components.

\textbf{7.} That connecting \omterm{def:g8:nervous-communication:neuron}{neurons}' voices from
elsewhere in the system reach even this loop's junction,
raising its readiness: the ``brainless'' reflex is still
wired into the whole, its \omterm{def:g8:nervous-communication:synapse}{synapse} hearing more than the
tendon.

\textbf{8.} The \omterm{def:g8:nervous-communication:synapse}{synapses} --- and the fibers' signalling
generally: the anesthetic quiets the chemistry at the
junctions and the message traffic with it, kick and
feeling together.

\textbf{9.} Report (tendon stretch) --- decide (one
cord junction) --- order (motor line): the loop with its
middle step reduced to a single \omterm{def:g8:nervous-communication:synapse}{synapse}, the \omterm{def:g5:brain-in-command:system}{brain}'s
weighing bypassed.

\textbf{10.} Reflex: tendon-cord-muscle, one junction, a
fiftieth of a second, unsuppressible, practice-free.
Catch: eye-brain-arm, uncounted junctions, a fifth of a
second, fully voluntary, practice-built.

\textbf{11.} Because the \omterm{def:g5:brain-in-command:system}{brain}'s weighing costs time, and
a burn deepens by the millisecond: loops that protect
tissue are soldered short, cord-level, and inform the
\omterm{def:g5:brain-in-command:system}{brain} afterward --- feel it late, saved already.

\textbf{12.} ``One hammer tap showed the one-word
frequency language, the two-line wiring through the cord,
and a junction that decides faster than its owner can.''
\end{solution}
