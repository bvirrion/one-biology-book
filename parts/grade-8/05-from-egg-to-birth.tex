\chapter{From Egg Cell to Birth}\label{ch:g8:from-egg-to-birth}

\Cref{ch:g5:human-reproduction} told the nine months as a
story; you now own every instrument the story needs played
properly: \omterm{def:g5:first-look-at-cells:cell}{cells} and their divisions, the two \omterm{def:g8:sexual-reproduction:gametes}{gametes} and
their meeting place, \omterm{def:g8:puberty:hormones}{hormones}, the \omterm{prop:g4:journey-of-food:absorb}{blood}'s deliveries. This
chapter replays human development with the machinery showing
--- from one \omterm{def:g5:first-look-at-cells:cell}{cell} to three thousand billion, in forty weeks.

\section{The first week: from zygote to lodger}

\begin{proposition}[The journey down the oviduct]\label{prop:g8:from-egg-to-birth:firstweek}
\omterm{def:g5:flower-to-fruit:steps}{Fertilization} in the \omterm{def:g8:reproductive-systems:female}{oviduct}
(\cref{def:g8:reproductive-systems:female}) starts two
clocks at once:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \emph{division}: the \omterm{prop:g8:sexual-reproduction:core}{zygote} divides within a day,
        then again and again
        (\cref{ex:g5:first-look-at-cells:one}) --- two,
        four, eight \omterm{def:g5:first-look-at-cells:cell}{cells}, a mulberry-ball of dozens by
        day four, all while drifting down the \omterm{def:g8:reproductive-systems:female}{oviduct}
        toward the uterus;
  \item \emph{implantation}: around day seven the little
        ball reaches the uterus and embeds itself in the
        prepared lining
        (\cref{prop:g8:reproductive-systems:cycle}'s event
        4) --- the lodger arrives, the cycle stops, and
        \omterm{def:g8:puberty:hormones}{hormones} signal the whole system: \omterm{def:g5:human-reproduction:pregnancy}{pregnancy}.
\end{enumerate}
\end{proposition}
\admitted

\begin{example}[What the first divisions live on]\label{ex:g8:from-egg-to-birth:provisions}
Through the first week the traveler eats no meal and
receives no delivery: it runs on the \omterm{def:g4:animal-reproduction:sexual}{egg cell}'s own
provisions --- exactly what
\cref{ex:g8:reproductive-systems:egg}'s stocking was for.
The sperm brought the father's \omterm{def:g6:cell-unit-of-life:parts}{nucleus} and little else;
the \omterm{def:g2:animals-grow-up:born}{egg} brought the other \omterm{def:g6:cell-unit-of-life:parts}{nucleus} \emph{and} the pantry.
Implantation is, among other things, the pantry running
low and the lodger plugging into room service.
\end{example}

\section{Embryo, placenta, fetus}

\begin{definition}[The placenta]\label{def:g8:from-egg-to-birth:placenta}
From the implanted ball grow two constructions at once:
the \emph{\omterm{def:g5:human-reproduction:pregnancy}{embryo}} itself, and its life-support interface
--- the \emph{placenta}\index{placenta}, a disc grown
into the uterus wall where the mother's \omterm{prop:g4:journey-of-food:absorb}{blood} and the
\omterm{def:g5:human-reproduction:pregnancy}{embryo}'s \omterm{prop:g4:journey-of-food:absorb}{blood} come intimately close \emph{without
mixing}. Across its vast thin surfaces --- the
specification of \cref{prop:g7:how-animals-breathe:spec},
built once more --- \omterm{def:g7:digestion-and-nutrients:families}{nutrients} and \omterm{def:g7:what-is-respiration:respiration}{oxygen} cross to the
\omterm{def:g5:human-reproduction:pregnancy}{embryo}, wastes and \omterm{def:g7:what-is-respiration:respiration}{carbon dioxide} cross back
(\cref{prop:g5:human-reproduction:cord}'s cord runs
between placenta and \omterm{def:g5:human-reproduction:pregnancy}{embryo}). The placenta is \omterm{def:g7:how-animals-breathe:four}{lung},
intestine and \omterm{def:g7:removing-wastes:kidneys}{kidney} to the passenger --- all three
borders in one organ.
\end{definition}

\begin{proposition}[The timetable, revisited with instruments]\label{prop:g8:from-egg-to-birth:timetable}
\Cref{ex:g5:human-reproduction:timetable}'s milestones,
now explained:
\begin{itemize}
  \item weeks 1--8, the \emph{\omterm{def:g5:human-reproduction:pregnancy}{embryo}}: the body plan is
        laid out --- \omterm{def:g5:first-look-at-cells:cell}{cells} divide, \omterm{def:g3:animals-through-seasons:migration}{migrate} and
        \emph{specialize}
        (\cref{rem:g6:cell-unit-of-life:twoways}) into
        \omterm{def:g4:heart-and-blood:heart}{heart}, \omterm{def:g5:brain-in-command:system}{nerve}, \omterm{def:g3:bones-and-muscles:skeleton}{bone} and the rest; the \omterm{def:g4:heart-and-blood:heart}{heart} beats
        from week four, because the pantry-and-placenta
        economy needs its pump early;
  \item weeks 9--40, the \emph{\omterm{def:g5:human-reproduction:pregnancy}{fetus}}: the plan built,
        growth and ripening --- the \omterm{def:g5:brain-in-command:system}{brain} wiring itself,
        \omterm{def:g7:how-animals-breathe:four}{lungs} rehearsing empty
        (\cref{exo:g5:human-reproduction:11} explained
        their first-cry debut), the kicks of month four
        announcing working \omterm{def:g3:bones-and-muscles:muscle}{muscles};
  \item throughout: \omterm{def:g8:puberty:hormones}{hormones} hold the uterus quiet and
        the cycle suspended --- the chemistry of
        \cref{def:g8:puberty:hormones}, running
        \omterm{def:g5:human-reproduction:pregnancy}{pregnancy}'s administration.
\end{itemize}
\end{proposition}
\admitted

\begin{example}[Why the early weeks ask the most care]\label{ex:g8:from-egg-to-birth:earlycare}
\Cref{prop:g5:human-reproduction:care} warned that
tobacco and alcohol cross to the baby; the timetable
says when it matters most: in the plan-laying \omterm{def:g5:human-reproduction:pregnancy}{embryo}
weeks, when each organ is a small crowd of specializing
\omterm{def:g5:first-look-at-cells:cell}{cells}, one disturbed instruction can echo through the
whole build --- and those are precisely the weeks a
\omterm{def:g5:human-reproduction:pregnancy}{pregnancy} can pass unnoticed. Hence the doctors' blunt
arithmetic: care begins not at the first scan but at the
first possibility.
\end{example}

\section{Birth, and the changeover}

\begin{proposition}[Birth as a hormone-ordered changeover]\label{prop:g8:from-egg-to-birth:birth}
Around week forty, \omterm{def:g8:puberty:hormones}{hormone} signals --- the baby's and
the mother's in dialogue --- end the uterus's long quiet:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \emph{labor}: the uterus, the body's strongest
        \omterm{def:g3:bones-and-muscles:muscle}{muscle} (\cref{rem:g8:reproductive-systems:living}
        met its gentler work), contracts in strengthening
        waves that open the way and push the baby down
        the \omterm{def:g2:born-grow-die:cycle}{birth} canal;
  \item \emph{\omterm{def:g2:born-grow-die:cycle}{birth}}: usually head first
        (\cref{ex:g5:human-reproduction:birth});
  \item \emph{the changeover}: the first cry fills the
        never-used \omterm{def:g7:how-animals-breathe:four}{lungs}; the cord's traffic ends and it
        is cut; within minutes the newborn runs on its
        own \omterm{def:g7:how-animals-breathe:four}{lungs}, gut-to-come and \omterm{def:g7:removing-wastes:kidneys}{kidneys} --- every
        border the \omterm{def:g8:from-egg-to-birth:placenta}{placenta} had covered
        (\cref{exo:g5:human-reproduction:11}, now with
        the full map);
  \item \emph{afterbirth}: the \omterm{def:g8:from-egg-to-birth:placenta}{placenta}, job done,
        detaches and follows --- the only organ the body
        builds for one use and discards.
\end{enumerate}
\end{proposition}
\admitted

\begin{example}[The newborn's first audit]\label{ex:g8:from-egg-to-birth:audit}
Midwives score a newborn minutes after \omterm{def:g2:born-grow-die:cycle}{birth} ---
\omterm{def:g7:what-is-respiration:respiration}{breathing}, \omterm{def:g4:heart-and-blood:heart}{heartbeat}, color, movement, cry. Read the
checklist with this book's \omterm{def:g1:the-five-senses:sense}{eyes}: it is
\cref{prop:g7:removing-wastes:complete}'s
supply-and-removal loop, audited at its first solo run.
The exam every human passes first is a physiology test.
\end{example}

\begin{method}[Explaining any stage of pregnancy]\label{met:g8:from-egg-to-birth:explain}
Asked about any moment of the nine months, answer with
four instruments:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \emph{\omterm{def:g5:first-look-at-cells:cell}{cells}}: what are the divisions and
        specializations doing?
  \item \emph{supply}: pantry, or \omterm{def:g8:from-egg-to-birth:placenta}{placenta} --- what
        crosses, what must not?
  \item \emph{\omterm{def:g8:puberty:hormones}{hormones}}: what is being held, or ordered?
  \item \emph{care}: what does the stage ask of the
        mother's habits and the doctors' calendar?
\end{enumerate}
\end{method}

\section{Problem: The Midwife's Timeline}

\begin{problem}[{Weekend problem --- a pregnancy followed
visit by visit}]\label{pb:g8:from-egg-to-birth:1}
A midwife's file for one (invented) \omterm{def:g5:human-reproduction:pregnancy}{pregnancy}: booking
visit at week 8, scans at weeks 12 and 20, checks at 28
and 36, \omterm{def:g2:born-grow-die:cycle}{birth} at week 40. Read the file with the
chapter's instruments.

\medskip\noindent\textbf{Part I --- The file's early
pages.}
\begin{enumerate}
  \item The \omterm{def:g5:human-reproduction:pregnancy}{pregnancy} was confirmed by a \omterm{def:g8:puberty:hormones}{hormone} test
        weeks before the first visit. Which signal does
        such a test read, and from which event of
        \cref{prop:g8:from-egg-to-birth:firstweek}
        onward does it exist?
  \item At week 8 the midwife records ``\omterm{def:g5:human-reproduction:pregnancy}{embryo} complete
        in plan, about the size of a bean''. Translate
        into the timetable's terms --- what phase closes
        here?
  \item The booking visit's advice leads with no
        alcohol, no smoke --- and folic acid, a builder's
        vitamin, ``ideally from before the start''.
        Justify all three from
        \cref{ex:g8:from-egg-to-birth:earlycare}.
  \item Week 12's scan shows the \omterm{def:g4:heart-and-blood:heart}{heartbeat} that began at
        week 4. Why so early, in supply terms?
\end{enumerate}

\medskip\noindent\textbf{Part II --- The middle months.}
\begin{enumerate}[resume]
  \item Week 20's scan measures every organ and the
        \omterm{def:g8:from-egg-to-birth:placenta}{placenta}'s position. Give the \omterm{def:g5:human-reproduction:pregnancy}{fetus} phase's
        business, and the \omterm{def:g8:from-egg-to-birth:placenta}{placenta}'s three jobs being
        checked.
  \item The mother reports the first kicks near week
        18. What machinery is announcing itself
        (\cref{prop:g5:brain-in-command:loop} names the
        chain)?
  \item At week 28 the midwife checks the mother's
        \omterm{prop:g4:journey-of-food:absorb}{blood} for sugar and pressure. Why is the
        mother's circulation doubly loaded --- whose
        borders is it running?
  \item Weeks 30--36: the \omterm{def:g5:human-reproduction:pregnancy}{fetus} mainly grows and the
        \omterm{def:g7:how-animals-breathe:four}{lungs} rehearse. Rehearse what --- and for which
        single moment?
\end{enumerate}

\medskip\noindent\textbf{Part III --- The last page.}
\begin{enumerate}[resume]
  \item Week 40: labor. Name the \omterm{def:g3:bones-and-muscles:muscle}{muscle}, its two tasks,
        and who ordered the work
        (\cref{prop:g8:from-egg-to-birth:birth}).
  \item The file's \omterm{def:g2:born-grow-die:cycle}{birth} entry: ``strong cry at once;
        cord clamped and cut; \omterm{def:g8:from-egg-to-birth:placenta}{placenta} complete.''
        Audit the three clauses with the changeover.
  \item The newborn scores high on the minutes-scale
        checklist. State what the checklist certifies,
        in \cref{ex:g8:from-egg-to-birth:audit}'s
        terms.
  \item Close the file with one sentence for the
        parents: what the forty weeks built, from what
        single \omterm{def:g5:first-look-at-cells:cell}{cell}, by which machinery.
\end{enumerate}
\end{problem}
