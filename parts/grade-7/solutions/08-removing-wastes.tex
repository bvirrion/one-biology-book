\section*{Chapter \ref{ch:g7:removing-wastes} --- Removing the Wastes}

\begin{solution}{exo:g7:removing-wastes:1}
A nitrogen-bearing waste left by the use of
building-matter \omterm{def:g7:digestion-and-nutrients:families}{nutrients} in the \omterm{def:g5:first-look-at-cells:cell}{cells}. Harmless in
passing traffic, it poisons if it piles up --- so it must
leave continuously.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g7:removing-wastes:2}
\omterm{def:g7:how-animals-breathe:four}{Lungs}: \omterm{def:g7:what-is-respiration:respiration}{carbon dioxide}. \omterm{def:g7:removing-wastes:kidneys}{Kidneys}: \omterm{prop:g7:removing-wastes:rubbish}{urea} and the surpluses of
water and salts. \omterm{def:g1:the-five-senses:sense}{Skin}: heat, and sweat's water and salts
(with traces of \omterm{prop:g7:removing-wastes:rubbish}{urea}).
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g7:removing-wastes:3}
Two, fist-sized and bean-shaped, low in the back, each fed
by a generous \omterm{def:g7:heart-and-circulation:vessels}{artery}. \omterm{def:g7:removing-wastes:kidneys}{Urine} trickles from each down its
tube to the \omterm{def:g7:removing-wastes:kidneys}{bladder} --- the holding tank emptied at will.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g7:removing-wastes:4}
First filter generously: many tens of litres of the
\omterm{prop:g4:journey-of-food:absorb}{blood}'s watery part are pushed through the sieves daily,
good and bad together. Then reclaim thriftily along the
winding tubes. The frame: tens of litres sieved, about a
litre and a half leaving as \omterm{def:g7:removing-wastes:kidneys}{urine}.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g7:removing-wastes:5}
Completely: \omterm{prop:g7:organs-at-work:bill}{glucose} (and nearly all the water). To
measure: water and salts, tuned to the day's needs. Not at
all: \omterm{prop:g7:removing-wastes:rubbish}{urea} --- the point of the whole exercise.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g7:removing-wastes:6}
The reclaiming is tuned: after much drinking, less water
is taken back --- plentiful pale \omterm{def:g7:removing-wastes:kidneys}{urine}; after sweating or
little drinking, nearly all is reclaimed --- scant dark
\omterm{def:g7:removing-wastes:kidneys}{urine}. The \omterm{def:g7:removing-wastes:kidneys}{kidney} guards the body's water account.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g7:removing-wastes:7}
\omterm{def:g7:what-is-respiration:respiration}{Carbon dioxide} is a gas, and gases cross at an air border
--- the \omterm{def:g7:lungs-and-gas-exchange:alveoli}{alveoli}; \omterm{prop:g7:removing-wastes:rubbish}{urea} is not a gas and cannot leave with
the breath, but it rides \omterm{def:g7:oxygen-in-the-water:dissolved}{dissolved} and filters beautifully
at a liquid border --- the sieves. Each waste exits by the
border its form suits: the division of labor is chemistry
matched to architecture.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g7:removing-wastes:8}
The sieve passes \omterm{prop:g7:organs-at-work:bill}{glucose} freely --- that is normal; the
reclaiming stage should then recover every unit of it.
\omterm{prop:g7:organs-at-work:bill}{Glucose} arriving in the \omterm{def:g7:removing-wastes:kidneys}{urine} means reclaiming was
overwhelmed or broken: either \omterm{prop:g4:journey-of-food:absorb}{blood} sugar so high the
\omterm{def:g7:organs-at-work:recovery}{recovery} cannot keep pace, or the \omterm{def:g7:organs-at-work:recovery}{recovery} itself failing
--- both worth a doctor's attention.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g7:removing-wastes:9}
\omterm{def:g3:bones-and-muscles:muscle}{Muscle} \omterm{def:g7:heart-and-circulation:vessels}{capillary} --- \omterm{def:g7:heart-and-circulation:vessels}{vein} --- right \omterm{def:g7:heart-and-circulation:rooms}{atrium} and \omterm{def:g7:heart-and-circulation:rooms}{ventricle}
--- \omterm{def:g7:how-animals-breathe:four}{lung} circuit (the \omterm{prop:g7:removing-wastes:rubbish}{urea} rides on; the \omterm{def:g7:how-animals-breathe:four}{lungs} cannot
touch it) --- left \omterm{def:g4:heart-and-blood:heart}{heart} --- body \omterm{def:g7:heart-and-circulation:vessels}{artery} to the \omterm{def:g7:removing-wastes:kidneys}{kidney} ---
sieved at a filter unit --- \emph{not} reclaimed --- down
the collecting tube, down to the \omterm{def:g7:removing-wastes:kidneys}{bladder}.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g7:removing-wastes:10}
Entry: no dismantling, crossing at the \omterm{prop:g7:digestion-and-nutrients:absorption}{villi} into the
\omterm{prop:g4:journey-of-food:absorb}{blood}. Transport: \omterm{prop:g7:digestion-and-nutrients:absorption}{liver} first, then both circuits.
Use: the medium of every \omterm{def:g5:first-look-at-cells:cell}{cell}'s wet chemistry, and the
cooling system's supply. Exit: shared among \omterm{def:g7:removing-wastes:kidneys}{urine} (tuned
by reclaiming), sweat (the sporty share), breath's
moisture, and a little in the leftovers.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g7:removing-wastes:11}
The sieve --- filtering wastes from \omterm{prop:g4:journey-of-food:absorb}{blood} across a fine
\omterm{def:g6:cell-unit-of-life:parts}{membrane}; the machine is an external filter unit. Sessions
recur because the \omterm{def:g5:first-look-at-cells:cell}{cells} never stop making \omterm{prop:g7:removing-wastes:rubbish}{urea}
(\cref{prop:g7:removing-wastes:rubbish}): a filter that
runs twice a week must catch up on days of accumulation,
where the \omterm{def:g7:removing-wastes:kidneys}{kidneys}' sieves run every minute.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g7:removing-wastes:12}
For example: one \omterm{prop:g4:journey-of-food:absorb}{blood}, driven round the \omterm{def:g7:how-animals-breathe:four}{lung} and body
circuits by the four-roomed \omterm{def:g4:heart-and-blood:heart}{heart}, loads \omterm{def:g7:what-is-respiration:respiration}{oxygen} at the
\omterm{def:g7:lungs-and-gas-exchange:alveoli}{alveoli} and \omterm{def:g7:digestion-and-nutrients:families}{nutrients} at the \omterm{prop:g7:digestion-and-nutrients:absorption}{villi}, and delivers both to
every \omterm{def:g5:first-look-at-cells:cell}{cell}'s \omterm{def:g7:heart-and-circulation:vessels}{capillaries}, where the slow fire of
\omterm{def:g7:what-is-respiration:respiration}{respiration} burns fuel with \omterm{def:g7:what-is-respiration:respiration}{oxygen} to power all work. The
same \omterm{prop:g4:journey-of-food:absorb}{blood} collects the fire's ashes --- \omterm{def:g7:what-is-respiration:respiration}{carbon dioxide}
to the \omterm{def:g7:lungs-and-gas-exchange:alveoli}{alveoli}, \omterm{prop:g7:removing-wastes:rubbish}{urea} and surpluses to the \omterm{def:g7:removing-wastes:kidneys}{kidney}'s
sieve-and-reclaim filters --- so the four borders serve
one economy. Every organ of the year is a station of
this loop, and the loop exists so that the fire in the
\omterm{def:g5:first-look-at-cells:cell}{cells} never lacks fuel, \omterm{def:g7:what-is-respiration:respiration}{oxygen}, or rubbish collection.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{pb:g7:removing-wastes:1}
\textbf{1.} Crossing at the \omterm{prop:g7:digestion-and-nutrients:absorption}{villi} of the \omterm{def:g4:journey-of-food:tract}{small intestine},
into the \omterm{def:g7:heart-and-circulation:vessels}{capillaries} --- the \omterm{prop:g4:journey-of-food:absorb}{blood} network receives it.

\textbf{2.} The \omterm{prop:g7:digestion-and-nutrients:absorption}{liver}. The routing serves the \omterm{def:g7:digestion-and-nutrients:families}{nutrients}
sharing the same \omterm{prop:g4:journey-of-food:absorb}{blood} --- sorting and banking --- and
water simply rides the standard line through it.

\textbf{3.} The \omterm{def:g7:how-animals-breathe:four}{lung} circuit and the body circuit, in
strict alternation through the four-roomed \omterm{def:g4:heart-and-blood:heart}{heart}.

\textbf{4.} It is the medium of the \omterm{def:g5:first-look-at-cells:cell}{cells}' chemistry ---
juices and burning run \omterm{def:g7:oxygen-in-the-water:dissolved}{dissolved} --- and the carrier of
everything the \omterm{prop:g4:journey-of-food:absorb}{blood} transports; it also feeds the
cooling reserve the afternoon will spend as sweat.

\textbf{5.} The \omterm{def:g1:the-five-senses:sense}{skin}: sweat --- water and salts --- whose
real job that day is cooling the furnace-hot working
\omterm{def:g3:bones-and-muscles:muscle}{muscles} (\cref{rem:g7:organs-at-work:warmth}).

\textbf{6.} Sweating spent the water account; the
\omterm{def:g7:removing-wastes:kidneys}{kidneys}' reclaiming stage answered by taking nearly all
sieved water back --- scant, concentrated, dark \omterm{def:g7:removing-wastes:kidneys}{urine} ---
while thirst demanded the account be refilled.

\textbf{7.} \omterm{def:g7:what-is-respiration:respiration}{Carbon dioxide}: \omterm{def:g3:bones-and-muscles:muscle}{muscle} \omterm{def:g7:heart-and-circulation:vessels}{capillary}, \omterm{def:g7:heart-and-circulation:vessels}{vein}, right
\omterm{def:g4:heart-and-blood:heart}{heart}, \omterm{def:g7:lungs-and-gas-exchange:alveoli}{alveoli} --- breathed out. \omterm{prop:g7:removing-wastes:rubbish}{Urea}: the same return,
then past the \omterm{def:g7:how-animals-breathe:four}{lungs}, left \omterm{def:g4:heart-and-blood:heart}{heart}, \omterm{def:g7:removing-wastes:kidneys}{kidney} \omterm{def:g7:heart-and-circulation:vessels}{artery}, sieved
and sent bladder-ward.

\textbf{8.} \omterm{prop:g7:removing-wastes:rubbish}{Urea} and the salts of sweat-changed \omterm{prop:g4:journey-of-food:absorb}{blood}:
waste production never pauses, so the sieves never may.
\omterm{def:g7:removing-wastes:kidneys}{Urine} \emph{volume} fell --- reclaiming kept the water
--- but the cleaning ran on.

\textbf{9.} The alveolar surface is moist, and each
breath sweeps warmed, moistened air out --- the misted
mirror's water. Part of any glass \omterm{def:g1:plants-around-us:parts}{leaves} as breath's
\omterm{def:g6:exploring-our-environment:conditions}{humidity}.

\textbf{10.} Entry: \omterm{prop:g7:digestion-and-nutrients:absorption}{villi}. Stores and uses: \omterm{prop:g4:journey-of-food:absorb}{blood}
volume, \omterm{def:g5:first-look-at-cells:cell}{cell} chemistry, cooling reserve. Exits: \omterm{def:g7:removing-wastes:kidneys}{urine}
(reclaim-tuned), sweat (the match's share), breath's
moisture, and a small share never absorbed, out with the
leftovers.

\textbf{11.} Because every organ draws supplies from,
and unloads wastes into, the one shared \omterm{prop:g4:journey-of-food:absorb}{blood}: a failing
\omterm{def:g7:how-animals-breathe:four}{lung} starves every \omterm{def:g3:bones-and-muscles:muscle}{muscle}, a failing \omterm{def:g7:removing-wastes:kidneys}{kidney} poisons
every \omterm{def:g5:first-look-at-cells:cell}{cell}, a furred \omterm{def:g7:heart-and-circulation:vessels}{artery} throttles whichever station
lies beyond it. One network means one fate, station by
station.

\textbf{12.} \omterm{def:g7:what-is-respiration:respiration}{Respiration} --- the slow fire in every
\omterm{def:g5:first-look-at-cells:cell}{cell}: entry, transport, burning, building and every
waste border exist to feed it, supply it, and carry away
its ashes.
\end{solution}
