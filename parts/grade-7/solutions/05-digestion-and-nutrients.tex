\section*{Chapter \ref{ch:g7:digestion-and-nutrients} --- Digestion and Nutrients}

\begin{solution}{exo:g7:digestion-and-nutrients:1}
They dismantle food \emph{chemically}: cutting its large
components --- starch, building matter, fats --- into
small, blood-ready units. Teeth and churning only make
pieces smaller; the juices change what the pieces are.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g7:digestion-and-nutrients:2}
Two tubes of starch paste at body warmth: one with fresh
\omterm{def:g4:journey-of-food:tract}{saliva}, one with boiled \omterm{def:g4:journey-of-food:tract}{saliva}. After an hour the fresh
tube's starch has become sugars; the boiled tube is
unchanged. Conclusions: the juice alone dismantles starch
--- no mouth needed --- and boiling destroys its working
substance.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g7:digestion-and-nutrients:3}
Sugars (\omterm{prop:g7:organs-at-work:bill}{glucose}) --- from starchy and sweet foods, like
bread; building-matter units --- from meat, \omterm{prop:g3:sorting-living-things:groups}{fish}, \omterm{def:g2:animals-grow-up:born}{eggs},
dairy, \omterm{prop:g4:heart-and-blood:pulse}{pulses}; fat units --- from oils and butter; plus
vitamins, \omterm{prop:g4:what-plants-need:needs}{mineral salts} and water --- from \omterm{prop:g3:flowers-fruits-seeds:transform}{fruit} and
vegetables among others.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g7:digestion-and-nutrients:4}
Vitamins, \omterm{prop:g4:what-plants-need:needs}{mineral salts} and water cross as they are.
Fibre defeats our juices entirely: uncut, it crosses
nothing and travels on as the leftovers' useful sweeping
bulk.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g7:digestion-and-nutrients:5}

about a millimetre high, one of millions. Each wraps its

\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g7:digestion-and-nutrients:6}
To the \omterm{prop:g7:digestion-and-nutrients:absorption}{liver} --- the sorting-and-storing organ. One
service: banking part of the meal's \omterm{prop:g7:organs-at-work:bill}{glucose} for later
release.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g7:digestion-and-nutrients:7}
Transport: food's large components cannot cross the
intestine's wall --- the wardrobe and the letterbox. And
reassembly: the body builds its own substance from small
units, so it wants parts, not furniture --- flat-pack for
the journey and for the rebuild.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g7:digestion-and-nutrients:8}

everything but the juice's working state. Its unchanged

--- and shows the working substance is heat-fragile,
destroyed by boiling like delicate machinery.
\end{solution}

\end{solution}

\omterm{def:g2:animals-grow-up:born}{Egg}: building-matter family. Dismantling starts in the
\omterm{def:g4:journey-of-food:tract}{stomach}'s acid juice (the cubes' fate in the glass gut),

Crossing: \omterm{prop:g7:digestion-and-nutrients:absorption}{villi}, \omterm{prop:g4:journey-of-food:absorb}{blood}, \omterm{prop:g7:digestion-and-nutrients:absorption}{liver}. Delivery: to the healing

\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g7:digestion-and-nutrients:11}
The building-matter units run short. First to suffer is
everything under construction and renewal: growth
itself, \omterm{def:g3:bones-and-muscles:muscle}{muscle} upkeep, repairs, and the constantly
renewed linings and \omterm{prop:g4:journey-of-food:absorb}{blood} components --- \omterm{def:g7:digestion-and-nutrients:families}{fuel-nutrients}
cannot substitute, because bricks and firewood are not
interchangeable.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g7:digestion-and-nutrients:12}
For example: a meal enters as furniture --- starch,
building matter, fat. Station by station its juices cut
each into its units: sugars, building units, fat units
--- the delivery list. At the \omterm{prop:g7:digestion-and-nutrients:absorption}{villi} the list crosses
into the \omterm{prop:g4:journey-of-food:absorb}{blood}, the \omterm{prop:g7:digestion-and-nutrients:absorption}{liver} sorts, banks and releases,
and the \omterm{prop:g4:journey-of-food:absorb}{blood} distributes: fuel to working \omterm{def:g3:bones-and-muscles:muscle}{muscles},
bricks to building sites, the rest to stores. What sat
on the plate as food circulates within hours as
deliveries with addresses.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{pb:g7:digestion-and-nutrients:1}
\textbf{1.} M: starch gone, sugars present. M$'$: starch
unchanged. S: cubes attacked --- shrunken, softened.
S$'$: cubes unchanged.

\textbf{2.} \omterm{def:g4:journey-of-food:tract}{Saliva}'s working substance dismantles starch
into sugars. M$'$ is the control: identical but for the
boiled juice, it proves the dismantler was the fresh
\omterm{def:g4:journey-of-food:tract}{saliva} and nothing else in the tube.

\textbf{3.} It has begun dismantling the \omterm{def:g2:animals-grow-up:born}{egg}'s building
matter --- cutting it toward its small units, the
building-matter \omterm{def:g7:digestion-and-nutrients:families}{nutrients}. (The clearing, shrinking
cubes are that cutting made visible.)

\textbf{4.} That the real work is chemical: with no
tooth and no churn in any tube, dismantling ran wherever
the right juice met the right food. Mechanical breaking
only serves the chemistry.

\textbf{5.} Juices are working substances with working
\omterm{def:g6:exploring-our-environment:conditions}{conditions}: chilled, they idle --- like every worker
from \omterm{def:g6:decomposers-and-soil:decomposers}{decomposer} to \omterm{ex:g6:producing-our-food:bread}{yeast}. The tube would sit undigested,
and prove only that fridges pause chemistry.

\textbf{6.} That each juice has its specialties and its
own station's \omterm{def:g6:exploring-our-environment:conditions}{conditions}: mixed in one tube, neither
works at its best, and the model loses the tract's
station-by-station order --- the very thing it is meant
to teach.

\textbf{7.} The absorbing wall: a villus-carpeted
surface. Its material must be thin (an easy crossing)
and selective-but-permeable to the small units --- with,
in the body, \omterm{def:g4:heart-and-blood:vessels}{blood vessels} behind it to carry the
crossers away.

\textbf{8.} Unchanged flakes: no juice of ours cuts
fibre. Meaning: the model correctly predicts fibre's
real fate --- crossing nothing, sweeping on --- the
exception that maps the system's limits.

\textbf{9.} Across the \omterm{prop:g7:digestion-and-nutrients:absorption}{villi}'s thin walls; into the
\omterm{prop:g4:journey-of-food:absorb}{blood}; through the \omterm{prop:g7:digestion-and-nutrients:absorption}{liver}, which banks part; out on
general delivery to the body's \omterm{def:g5:first-look-at-cells:cell}{cells} --- a working
\omterm{def:g3:bones-and-muscles:muscle}{muscle} burning them within the hour.

\textbf{10.} \omterm{def:g6:exploring-our-environment:conditions}{Conditions} are part of the machinery: each
station is tuned --- acid here, neutralized there --- so
the juices must meet food in the right order, which is
exactly what swallowing through the stations provides.
The tract is a production line, not a bucket.

\textbf{11.} Because \omterm{def:g4:journey-of-food:digestion}{digestion}'s workers are substances,
not organs: as the rack shows, a juice dismantles its
food wherever the two meet in the right \omterm{def:g6:exploring-our-environment:conditions}{conditions}. A
capsule delivering the missing juice to the right
station can therefore do the station's chemistry.

\textbf{12.} ``The juices, each at its station, cut food
into \omterm{def:g7:digestion-and-nutrients:families}{nutrients} --- and the control tubes proved it was
the juices, and nothing else, doing the cutting.''
\end{solution}
