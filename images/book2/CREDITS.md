## AI-generated illustrations (`ai/`)

The images under `images/book2/ai/` are AI-generated illustrations,
produced with OpenAI image generation driven through the Codex CLI
(`codex exec`), from editor-written prompts recorded in
`images/book2/ai/PROMPTS.md`, and visually reviewed for biological
accuracy before insertion into the book. They are disclosed collectively
on the book's Image Credits page (`frontmatter/image-credits-book2.tex`).
