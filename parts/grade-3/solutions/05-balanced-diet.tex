\section*{Chapter \ref{ch:g3:balanced-diet} --- A Balanced Diet}

\begin{solution}{exo:g3:balanced-diet:1}
Providing \omterm{prop:g3:balanced-diet:jobs}{energy}, and providing building material. \omterm{prop:g3:balanced-diet:jobs}{Energy}:
cereals and starchy foods (or fats). Building: meat, \omterm{prop:g3:sorting-living-things:groups}{fish} and
\omterm{def:g2:animals-grow-up:born}{eggs}, or dairy.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g3:balanced-diet:2}
\omterm{prop:g3:flowers-fruits-seeds:transform}{Fruit} and vegetables; cereals and starchy foods; dairy; meat,
\omterm{prop:g3:sorting-living-things:groups}{fish} and \omterm{def:g2:animals-grow-up:born}{eggs}; fats; sugary foods (the treat family). The one
required drink is water.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g3:balanced-diet:3}
Rice, bread: cereals/starchy. Yogurt: dairy. Carrots, apples:
\omterm{prop:g3:flowers-fruits-seeds:transform}{fruit} and vegetables. \omterm{def:g2:animals-grow-up:born}{Eggs}: meat-fish-eggs. Butter: fats.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g3:balanced-diet:4}
Because the body never stops: the heart beats all night, the
chest keeps breathing, and the body stays warm at about
$\qty{37}{\degreeCelsius}$ --- all paid for in \omterm{prop:g3:balanced-diet:jobs}{energy}.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g3:balanced-diet:5}
Green beans and the apple: the freshness half. Pasta: the
starchy quarter. \omterm{prop:g3:sorting-living-things:groups}{Fish} and the yogurt: the builders' quarter.
Water: the glass. Every part of the plate is served.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g3:balanced-diet:6}
The body has run all night on no deliveries; breakfast restocks
it. Without it, by mid-morning \omterm{prop:g3:balanced-diet:jobs}{energy} runs short: rumbling
stomach, wandering attention, weak play.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g3:balanced-diet:7}
The child --- a child's body is still building itself taller
and bigger, on top of repairing and running, so building
material matters proportionally more.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g3:balanced-diet:8}
Open answer. The commonly forgotten family is \omterm{prop:g3:flowers-fruits-seeds:transform}{fruit} and
vegetables; the one over its share, sugary foods.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g3:balanced-diet:9}
No food is forbidden: balance is judged over days, not bites.
The rule is that treats must stay small and must not take the
place of the families --- the question is ``which family have I
forgotten?'', not ``which food is bad?''.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g3:balanced-diet:10}
Sport spends extra \omterm{prop:g3:balanced-diet:jobs}{energy} in movement, and cold spends extra
\omterm{prop:g3:balanced-diet:jobs}{energy} keeping the body warm. Spending more, the body asks for
more: hunger grows with the day's expenses.
\end{solution}
