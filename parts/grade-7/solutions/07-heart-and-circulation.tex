\begin{solution}{pb:g7:heart-and-circulation:1}
\textbf{1.} The \omterm{prop:g4:heart-and-blood:pulse}{pulse}: the \omterm{def:g7:heart-and-circulation:vessels}{arteries}' surge count --- the
pump's rate read at a \omterm{def:g1:my-body:joint}{wrist}. \omterm{prop:g4:journey-of-food:absorb}{Blood} pressure: the \omterm{def:g7:heart-and-circulation:vessels}{artery}
pressure at surge and between --- the highways' load.
The stethoscope's lub-dub: the \omterm{def:g7:heart-and-circulation:rooms}{valves} heard closing ---
the doors' soundness.

\textbf{2.} A low resting \omterm{prop:g4:heart-and-blood:pulse}{pulse} in a fit patient means a
strong \omterm{def:g7:heart-and-circulation:rooms}{ventricle} moving more \omterm{prop:g4:journey-of-food:absorb}{blood} per beat: the
trained \omterm{def:g4:heart-and-blood:heart}{heart} idles calm, with reserve in hand.

\textbf{3.} The valve doors: entry \omterm{def:g7:heart-and-circulation:rooms}{valves} at the
\omterm{def:g7:heart-and-circulation:rooms}{ventricles}' drive (lub), exit \omterm{def:g7:heart-and-circulation:rooms}{valves} at their
relaxation (dub) --- four doors closing in two pairs,
cleanly.

\textbf{4.} Right \omterm{def:g7:heart-and-circulation:rooms}{atrium}, right \omterm{def:g7:heart-and-circulation:rooms}{ventricle}, \omterm{def:g7:how-animals-breathe:four}{lung}
\omterm{def:g7:heart-and-circulation:vessels}{capillaries}, left \omterm{def:g7:heart-and-circulation:rooms}{atrium}, left \omterm{def:g7:heart-and-circulation:rooms}{ventricle}, body
\omterm{def:g7:heart-and-circulation:vessels}{arteries}, body \omterm{def:g7:heart-and-circulation:vessels}{capillaries} (say, a \omterm{def:g3:bones-and-muscles:muscle}{muscle}), \omterm{def:g7:heart-and-circulation:vessels}{veins} ---
and home to the right \omterm{def:g7:heart-and-circulation:rooms}{atrium}.

\textbf{5.} Each beat loses part of its surge backward
through the leak, so the body circuit's deliveries per
beat shrink; the \omterm{def:g4:heart-and-blood:heart}{heart} compensates with more beats and
harder work, and stamina drops --- the murmur is
delivery lost as backwash.

\textbf{6.} \omterm{prop:g4:journey-of-food:absorb}{Blood} pressure up (stiff, narrowed highways
resist the surge) and the \omterm{prop:g4:heart-and-blood:pulse}{pulse}'s \omterm{def:g7:heart-and-circulation:vessels}{artery} stretch
changed; the left \omterm{def:g7:heart-and-circulation:rooms}{ventricle}, pushing every round
against the stiffened circuit, works overtime and
thickens.

\textbf{7.} Furring in the \omterm{def:g4:heart-and-blood:heart}{heart}'s own feeding
\omterm{def:g7:heart-and-circulation:vessels}{arteries} throttles the fuel line of the \omterm{def:g3:bones-and-muscles:muscle}{muscle} that
can never rest --- the classic path to a \omterm{def:g4:heart-and-blood:heart}{heart} attack.

\textbf{8.} The \omterm{def:g7:heart-and-circulation:vessels}{veins}' one-way \omterm{def:g7:heart-and-circulation:rooms}{valves} and the missing
muscle-pump of movement: standing still, the calves
never squeeze, the \omterm{def:g7:heart-and-circulation:rooms}{valves}' gains stall, and the \omterm{def:g1:my-body:joint}{ankles}
pool. Walking squeezes the \omterm{def:g7:heart-and-circulation:vessels}{veins} rhythmically --- the
relief is mechanical.

\textbf{9.} Because the body's tour spends the \omterm{prop:g4:journey-of-food:absorb}{blood}'s
\omterm{def:g7:what-is-respiration:respiration}{oxygen}: only the \omterm{def:g7:how-animals-breathe:four}{lung} circuit can reload it. Skipping
the \omterm{def:g7:how-animals-breathe:four}{lungs} would send spent \omterm{prop:g4:journey-of-food:absorb}{blood} on deliveries with an
empty van --- the double loop is refuel-then-deliver,
enforced by the \omterm{def:g4:heart-and-blood:heart}{heart}'s two sides.

\textbf{10.} \omterm{def:g7:heart-and-circulation:vessels}{Artery}: thick, elastic, high pressure ---
outbound highways. \omterm{def:g7:heart-and-circulation:vessels}{Capillary}: one \omterm{def:g5:first-look-at-cells:cell}{cell} thin, threading
everything --- the delivery lanes where all exchanges
happen. \omterm{def:g7:heart-and-circulation:vessels}{Vein}: wide, valved, low pressure --- the
return.

\textbf{11.} Every consultation's trouble mattered
exactly where it touched \omterm{def:g7:heart-and-circulation:vessels}{capillary} service: the leak
starved deliveries, the furring throttled supply
lines, the pooling stalled returns. Readings, \omterm{def:g7:heart-and-circulation:rooms}{valves}
and highways are means; \omterm{def:g7:heart-and-circulation:vessels}{capillary} traffic --- the
exchanges --- is the end.

\textbf{12.} Move daily --- the pump strengthens and
the highways stay supple; eat balanced --- the \omterm{def:g7:heart-and-circulation:vessels}{artery}
walls stay clear of furring; no smoke --- \omterm{def:g4:heart-and-blood:vessels}{vessels}
neither narrow nor stiffen, and the \omterm{prop:g4:journey-of-food:absorb}{blood}'s seats stay
free for \omterm{def:g7:what-is-respiration:respiration}{oxygen}.
\end{solution}
