\chapter{Digestion and Nutrients}\label{ch:g7:digestion-and-nutrients}

\Cref{ch:g4:journey-of-food} followed the bite of bread down
the tube and promised that ``\omterm{def:g4:journey-of-food:digestion}{digestive juices} dismantle it''.
This year we open the workshop doors: what exactly do the
juices do, what are the \omterm{def:g7:digestion-and-nutrients:families}{nutrients} they produce, and how does
the \omterm{def:g4:journey-of-food:tract}{small intestine}'s wall manage the greatest border
crossing in the body? The bite of bread is back --- under
better instruments.

\section{The juices at work}

\begin{proposition}[Digestion is chemical dismantling]\label{prop:g7:digestion-and-nutrients:chemical}
Chewing and churning only break food \emph{smaller}; the
real dismantling is chemical. The \emph{digestive
juices}\index{digestive juice} --- \omterm{def:g4:journey-of-food:tract}{saliva}, the \omterm{def:g4:journey-of-food:tract}{stomach}'s
juice, the juices poured into the \omterm{def:g4:journey-of-food:tract}{small intestine} ---
contain working substances that cut food's large components
into small ones: starch into sugars, meat and \omterm{def:g2:animals-grow-up:born}{eggs}'
building matter into its small units, fats into theirs.
Each juice has its specialties, and each works best at its
own station's \omterm{def:g6:exploring-our-environment:conditions}{conditions}.
\end{proposition}
\admitted

\begin{example}[The bench version]\label{ex:g7:digestion-and-nutrients:bench}
The classic experiment runs \omterm{def:g4:journey-of-food:digestion}{digestion} in glass. Two tubes
of cooked starch paste at body warmth: one gets fresh
\omterm{def:g4:journey-of-food:tract}{saliva}, one gets boiled (spoiled) \omterm{def:g4:journey-of-food:tract}{saliva}. An hour later,
the starch test finds tube 2 unchanged --- still starch ---
while tube 1's paste has turned to sugars, exactly the
sweetness of \cref{ex:g4:journey-of-food:sweet}, produced
without any mouth. The juice alone did the dismantling ---
and the boiled control shows its working substance is
destroyed by heat, as delicate machinery would be.
\end{example}

\begin{remark}[Why dismantle at all?]\label{rem:g7:digestion-and-nutrients:why}
Food's large components can no more cross the intestine's
wall than a wardrobe can pass a letterbox. And the body
does not want them whole: it wants \emph{parts} --- small
units it can rebuild into its own substance
(\cref{prop:g6:matter-from-food:transform}). \omterm{def:g4:journey-of-food:digestion}{Digestion} is
dismantling for transport \emph{and} for reassembly:
furniture to flat-pack, at every meal.
\end{remark}

\section{The nutrients}

\begin{definition}[The nutrient families]\label{def:g7:digestion-and-nutrients:families}
The dismantling yields the \emph{nutrients}\index{nutrient}
--- the small, blood-ready components:
\begin{itemize}
  \item \emph{sugars} (\omterm{prop:g7:organs-at-work:bill}{glucose} above all,
        \cref{prop:g7:organs-at-work:bill}'s fuel), from
        starchy and sweet foods;
  \item the small units of \emph{building matter}, from
        meat, \omterm{prop:g3:sorting-living-things:groups}{fish}, \omterm{def:g2:animals-grow-up:born}{eggs}, dairy and \omterm{prop:g4:heart-and-blood:pulse}{pulses} --- the bricks
        of \cref{prop:g3:balanced-diet:jobs}'s
        construction;
  \item the units of \emph{fats} --- dense fuel and
        building material both;
  \item plus passengers needing no dismantling:
        \emph{vitamins}, \emph{\omterm{prop:g4:what-plants-need:needs}{mineral salts}}, and water
        itself.
\end{itemize}
The \omterm{def:g3:balanced-diet:families}{food families} of \cref{def:g3:balanced-diet:families}
are, at bottom, suppliers of these nutrient families ---
the shopping list behind the shopping list.
\end{definition}

\begin{example}[One lunch, itemized]\label{ex:g7:digestion-and-nutrients:lunch}
The balanced lunch of \cref{ex:g3:balanced-diet:lunch},
re-read as \omterm{def:g7:digestion-and-nutrients:families}{nutrients}: pasta --- starch, dismantled to
\omterm{prop:g7:organs-at-work:bill}{glucose} by the litre; \omterm{prop:g3:sorting-living-things:groups}{fish} --- building matter, cut to its
units; the oil on the beans --- fat units; beans and apple
--- vitamins, minerals, water, plus fibre. Fibre, note, is
the exception that proves the system: no juice of ours can
cut it, so it crosses nothing and travels on --- the
leftovers' bulk (\cref{prop:g4:journey-of-food:absorb}),
doing its own useful sweeping.
\end{example}

\section{The great crossing}

\begin{proposition}[Absorption at the villi]\label{prop:g7:digestion-and-nutrients:absorption}
The crossing promised in
\cref{prop:g4:journey-of-food:absorb} happens across the
\omterm{def:g4:journey-of-food:tract}{small intestine}'s inner wall, and the wall is built for it:
folded, and carpeted with millions of
\emph{villi}\index{villus} --- finger-shaped bumps a
millimetre high, each wrapping its own fine \omterm{def:g4:heart-and-blood:vessels}{blood vessels}.
The \omterm{prop:g7:digestion-and-nutrients:absorption}{villi} are why the crossing surface reaches its
carpet-a-room size (\cref{rem:g4:journey-of-food:surface}):
folds on folds on fingers. \omterm{def:g7:digestion-and-nutrients:families}{Nutrients} cross their thin
walls into the \omterm{prop:g4:journey-of-food:absorb}{blood}, and the \omterm{prop:g4:journey-of-food:absorb}{blood} carries them first to
a great sorting-and-storing organ --- the
\emph{liver}\index{liver} --- before general delivery.
\end{proposition}
\admitted

\begin{example}[The specification again]\label{ex:g7:digestion-and-nutrients:spec}
Large, thin, moist, blood-lined: the \omterm{prop:g7:digestion-and-nutrients:absorption}{villi} meet exactly
\cref{prop:g7:how-animals-breathe:spec}'s specification ---
for \omterm{def:g7:digestion-and-nutrients:families}{nutrients} instead of \omterm{def:g7:what-is-respiration:respiration}{oxygen}. \omterm{def:g7:how-animals-breathe:four}{Lungs}' pockets, \omterm{def:g7:how-animals-breathe:four}{gills}'
\omterm{def:g1:animals-around-us:covering}{feathers}, intestine's \omterm{prop:g7:digestion-and-nutrients:absorption}{villi}: wherever the body must move
much across a border, it builds the same machine. Spot the
specification once, and you recognize it everywhere.
\end{example}

\begin{method}[Tracing a nutrient end to end]\label{met:g7:digestion-and-nutrients:trace}
For any mouthful, trace one \omterm{def:g7:digestion-and-nutrients:families}{nutrient}:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item name the food's family and its main components;
  \item station by station, note where its dismantling
        starts and finishes, and which juices worked on it;
  \item cross at the \omterm{prop:g7:digestion-and-nutrients:absorption}{villi} with it, into the \omterm{prop:g4:journey-of-food:absorb}{blood}, via
        the \omterm{prop:g7:digestion-and-nutrients:absorption}{liver};
  \item deliver it: to a working \omterm{def:g5:first-look-at-cells:cell}{cell} as fuel
        (\cref{prop:g7:organs-at-work:bill}), or to a
        building site as material
        (\cref{def:g6:matter-from-food:production}).
\end{enumerate}
\end{method}

\begin{example}[The trace, run on bread]\label{ex:g7:digestion-and-nutrients:breadtrace}
Bread: starchy family, mainly starch. Dismantling starts
in the mouth (\omterm{def:g4:journey-of-food:tract}{saliva} --- the long-chew sweetness),
pauses in the \omterm{def:g4:journey-of-food:tract}{stomach}'s acid, finishes in the \omterm{def:g4:journey-of-food:tract}{small
intestine}'s juices: \omterm{prop:g7:organs-at-work:bill}{glucose}. Crossing: \omterm{prop:g7:digestion-and-nutrients:absorption}{villi}, \omterm{prop:g4:journey-of-food:absorb}{blood},
\omterm{prop:g7:digestion-and-nutrients:absorption}{liver} --- which banks part of the \omterm{prop:g7:organs-at-work:bill}{glucose} for later, one
of its many services. Delivery: a thigh \omterm{def:g3:bones-and-muscles:muscle}{muscle} mid-sprint
burns it within the hour. The bite of grade four, fully
accounted.
\end{example}

\section{Problem: The Glass Gut}

\begin{problem}[{Weekend problem --- building digestion on
the bench, tube by tube}]\label{pb:g7:digestion-and-nutrients:1}
The class builds a ``glass gut'': a rack of tubes at body
warmth, each simulating one station of the tract. Tube M
(mouth): starch paste plus fresh \omterm{def:g4:journey-of-food:tract}{saliva}. Tube M$'$:
starch paste plus boiled \omterm{def:g4:journey-of-food:tract}{saliva}. Tube S (\omterm{def:g4:journey-of-food:tract}{stomach}): \omterm{def:g2:animals-grow-up:born}{egg}
white cubes plus \omterm{def:g4:journey-of-food:tract}{stomach} juice from the pharmacist. Tube
S$'$: the same cubes in plain water. All run for two
hours; then the tests --- for starch, for sugars, and a
look at the cubes.

\medskip\noindent\textbf{Part I --- Predictions and
results.}
\begin{enumerate}
  \item Predict the two-hour results of all four tubes.
  \item M turns sugary, M$'$ does not. State the
        conclusion, and the exact role of M$'$.
  \item S's cubes shrink, soften and clear; S$'$'s sit
        unchanged. What has the \omterm{def:g4:journey-of-food:tract}{stomach} juice done ---
        and what kind of \omterm{def:g7:digestion-and-nutrients:families}{nutrient} family is being
        produced?
  \item No tube was chewed or churned. What does the
        rack prove about where \omterm{def:g4:journey-of-food:digestion}{digestion}'s real work
        lies?
\end{enumerate}

\medskip\noindent\textbf{Part II --- Improving the model.}
\begin{enumerate}[resume]
  \item A student proposes running tube M at fridge
        \omterm{def:g6:exploring-our-environment:conditions}{temperature} ``for tidiness''. Object, with
        \cref{prop:g6:decomposers-and-soil:speed}'s
        pattern applied to juices.
  \item Another proposes one big tube with both juices
        mixed. What station-by-station fact of
        \cref{prop:g7:digestion-and-nutrients:chemical}
        would the mixture blur?
  \item The glass gut has no wall: nothing is absorbed.
        Which structure would a full model need next,
        and what two properties must its material have?
  \item Add fibre --- bran flakes --- to a fifth tube
        with every juice available. Predict its two-hour
        state, and its meaning for the model.
\end{enumerate}

\medskip\noindent\textbf{Part III --- From glass to gut.}
\begin{enumerate}[resume]
  \item In the body, M's sugars would next meet the
        \omterm{prop:g7:digestion-and-nutrients:absorption}{villi}. Continue their journey in four stops.
  \item The \omterm{def:g4:journey-of-food:tract}{stomach}'s juice works in strong acid; the
        intestine's juices need that acid neutralized.
        What does this say about ``each juice at its own
        station's \omterm{def:g6:exploring-our-environment:conditions}{conditions}'' --- and about swallowing
        the stations in order?
  \item A pharmaceutical company sells juice capsules
        for people whose own stations underperform.
        Explain, with the rack, why such a therapy is
        even possible.
  \item Summarize the practical in one sentence
        containing \emph{juices}, \emph{\omterm{def:g7:digestion-and-nutrients:families}{nutrients}} and
        \emph{control tubes}.
\end{enumerate}
\end{problem}
