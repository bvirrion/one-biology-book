# AI image prompts — Book 4

Every image in this directory was generated with OpenAI image generation
driven through the Codex CLI (`codex exec`, agent model `gpt-6-astra`,
built-in image tool) on 2026-09-10 and 2026-09-11, from the prompt recorded
below (aspect ratio noted), and visually reviewed for biological accuracy
before insertion into the book. Rejected generations are not kept; two
images (`b4-10-frog-development`, `b4-19-adrenaline-runner`) were cropped
after generation to remove empty margins.

Shared style suffix appended to every prompt: *"Clean realistic scientific illustration style, natural colours, soft even lighting, plain white or neutral background, absolutely no text, no letters, no labels, no arrows, no numbers, no captions."*

Book 4 also reuses two photographs from earlier volumes (see
`images/book4/CREDITS.md`); no Book 2 or Book 3 AI base is reused.
