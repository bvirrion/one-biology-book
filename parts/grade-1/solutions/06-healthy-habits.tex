\section*{Chapter \ref{ch:g1:healthy-habits} --- Healthy Habits}

\begin{solution}{exo:g1:healthy-habits:1}
\omterm{def:g1:healthy-habits:germs}{Germs} are \omterm{def:g1:living-or-not:living}{living things} far too small to see, found everywhere;
some can make us ill when they get inside the body. Because they
are invisible, hands can look perfectly clean and still carry
them.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g1:healthy-habits:2}
Before eating, after the toilet, and after playing outside or
touching \omterm{def:g1:animals-around-us:animal}{animals}.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g1:healthy-habits:3}
While slowly counting to $20$ (about a short song). Easy to
forget: between the fingers, around the thumbs, and the backs of
the hands.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g1:healthy-habits:4}
Bits of food stay on the teeth after eating. \omterm{def:g1:healthy-habits:germs}{Germs} in the mouth
feed on these leftovers, especially sugary ones, and little by
little they make holes in the teeth, which hurt. Brushing sweeps
the leftovers away before the feast.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g1:healthy-habits:5}
After breakfast and before bed, every day --- and it should take
about as long as a song, on every side of every tooth.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g1:healthy-habits:6}
\omterm{prop:g1:healthy-habits:needs}{Varied food} (fruit at breakfast, water to drink), movement
(playing outside, cycling), and \omterm{prop:g1:healthy-habits:needs}{sleep} (a long night with an early
bedtime). Examples open.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g1:healthy-habits:7}
It rests, repairs itself and grows. A child needs the longer night
--- far longer than a grown-up's.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g1:healthy-habits:8}
For example: yawning, stinging or heavy \omterm{def:g1:the-five-senses:sense}{eyes}, grumpiness for no
reason.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g1:healthy-habits:9}
Baby teeth must chew for years before they fall out; holes in
them hurt just as much as in grown-up teeth; and the grown-up
teeth waiting underneath deserve a clean, healthy mouth to arrive
in. Two of these reasons suffice.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g1:healthy-habits:10}
The glitter stands for \omterm{def:g1:healthy-habits:germs}{germs}. Shaking hands, opening doors and
sharing pencils passes them invisibly from hand to hand --- and
washing with soap for a slow count of $20$ removes them, glitter
and \omterm{def:g1:healthy-habits:germs}{germs} alike.
\end{solution}
