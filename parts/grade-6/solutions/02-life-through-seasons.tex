\section*{Chapter \ref{ch:g6:life-through-seasons} --- Life Through the Seasons}

\begin{solution}{exo:g6:life-through-seasons:1}
Day length (\omterm{def:g6:exploring-our-environment:conditions}{light}), \omterm{def:g6:exploring-our-environment:conditions}{temperature}, and soil or air dampness
following rain and drought.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g6:life-through-seasons:2}
\omterm{def:g6:life-through-seasons:trees}{Deciduous} --- drops all its \omterm{def:g1:plants-around-us:parts}{leaves} in autumn: oak, beech,
horse chestnut. \omterm{def:g6:life-through-seasons:trees}{Evergreen} --- keeps working \omterm{def:g1:plants-around-us:parts}{leaves} all
winter: pine, holly.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g6:life-through-seasons:3}
A tiny complete shoot --- miniature folded \omterm{def:g1:plants-around-us:parts}{leaves} wrapped in
weatherproof scales. It was built the summer before.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g6:life-through-seasons:4}
The annual dies entirely and passes winter as \omterm{prop:g3:flowers-fruits-seeds:transform}{seeds}. The
daffodil's top dies back while the \omterm{def:g1:plants-around-us:plant}{plant} waits underground
as a \omterm{ex:g4:plants-without-seeds:bulbtuber}{bulb}.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g6:life-through-seasons:5}
Because the shoots inside its \omterm{def:g6:life-through-seasons:buds}{buds} were already finished
last summer; the twig lacked only warmth, which the indoor
water supplies weeks before the garden does.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g6:life-through-seasons:6}
\omterm{def:g3:animals-through-seasons:migration}{Migrate}: the swallow. \omterm{def:g3:animals-through-seasons:hibernation}{Hibernate}: the hedgehog (or the sealed
snail). \omterm{def:g3:animals-through-seasons:active}{Stay active}: the fox, the magpie, the robin. Winter
as young stages --- \omterm{def:g2:animals-grow-up:born}{egg}, larva or \omterm{ex:g2:animals-grow-up:butterfly}{chrysalis}: most \omterm{prop:g3:sorting-living-things:groups}{insects},
like the grasshopper or the butterfly.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g6:life-through-seasons:7}
Butterfly: a \omterm{ex:g2:animals-grow-up:butterfly}{chrysalis} under a coping stone. Grasshopper:
\omterm{def:g2:animals-grow-up:born}{eggs} in the soil. Ladybug: a sleeping adult huddled deep in
\omterm{def:g1:plants-around-us:parts}{leaf} litter. Snail: sealed in its \omterm{def:g1:animals-around-us:covering}{shell} in the bank. Swallow:
in Africa, as its migrated self.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g6:life-through-seasons:8}
So that any difference between the four tables comes from
the seasons and not from the surveying --- the fair-test
rule of \cref{met:g6:exploring-our-environment:survey},
applied across time instead of across sites.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g6:life-through-seasons:9}
First, many ``absentees'' are present in hidden forms ---
\omterm{def:g2:animals-grow-up:born}{eggs}, chrysalises, sleeping adults, \omterm{ex:g4:plants-without-seeds:bulbtuber}{bulbs} --- that a
walking-about survey misses without digging and lifting.
Second, some are not lost but elsewhere: the \omterm{def:g3:animals-through-seasons:migration}{migrators} will
return. The January list understates residents and counts
travelers as vanished.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g6:life-through-seasons:10}
The \omterm{def:g1:plants-around-us:plant}{plant} is in its underground storage state --- alive,
waiting as \omterm{ex:g4:plants-without-seeds:bulbtuber}{bulbs}. In March the \omterm{ex:g4:plants-without-seeds:bulbtuber}{bulbs} will send up \omterm{def:g1:plants-around-us:parts}{leaves} and
\omterm{def:g3:flowers-fruits-seeds:parts}{flowers} again, denser if the clumps have multiplied.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g6:life-through-seasons:11}
Gone where, as what? Frogs winter dormant --- buried in the
pond's mud bottom or in damp shelters on land --- passing
the cold months torpid until spring calls them back to the
water to breed.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g6:life-through-seasons:12}
Winter's problem for a working \omterm{def:g1:plants-around-us:parts}{leaf} is holding water: frozen
ground gives \omterm{def:g1:plants-around-us:parts}{roots} almost none, while wind and sun keep
drawing it out --- the woodlouse's drying problem, all
winter long. The \omterm{def:g6:life-through-seasons:trees}{evergreen} \omterm{def:g1:plants-around-us:parts}{leaf}'s wax and needle build cuts
those losses to what the tree can afford. A broad thin \omterm{def:g1:plants-around-us:parts}{leaf}
cannot be sealed so well; the \omterm{def:g6:life-through-seasons:trees}{deciduous} tree's cheaper
answer is to write its thin \omterm{def:g1:plants-around-us:parts}{leaves} off each autumn and
store spring in \omterm{def:g6:life-through-seasons:buds}{buds} instead.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{pb:g6:life-through-seasons:1}
\textbf{1.} The \omterm{def:g6:life-through-seasons:buds}{buds} on every twig --- winter storage
holding next spring's shoots, built the previous summer.

\textbf{2.} Between the March and April frames (with the
notes' lengthening days and milder mornings). The tree
answers the rising \omterm{def:g6:exploring-our-environment:conditions}{temperature} and lengthening \omterm{def:g6:exploring-our-environment:conditions}{light}.

\textbf{3.} Pollinating \omterm{prop:g3:sorting-living-things:groups}{insects} --- bees above all --- must
visit the \omterm{def:g3:flowers-fruits-seeds:parts}{flowers}, so that \omterm{def:g3:flowers-fruits-seeds:parts}{pollen} reaches \omterm{def:g3:flowers-fruits-seeds:parts}{pistils} and
\omterm{def:g5:flower-to-fruit:steps}{fertilization} follows. A conker is the \omterm{def:g2:from-seed-to-plant:seed}{seed} formed from a
fertilized ovule, in its spiky \omterm{prop:g3:flowers-fruits-seeds:transform}{fruit} case.

\textbf{4.} \omterm{def:g6:life-through-seasons:trees}{Deciduous}. Its next spring rides the winter
frames as \omterm{def:g6:life-through-seasons:buds}{buds} on the bare twigs.

\textbf{5.} Below the lawn, as \omterm{ex:g4:plants-without-seeds:bulbtuber}{bulbs} --- the perennial's
underground storage.

\textbf{6.} As \omterm{prop:g3:flowers-fruits-seeds:transform}{seeds} in the soil: the annual's strategy ---
the \omterm{def:g1:plants-around-us:plant}{plants} themselves died in autumn.

\textbf{7.} The swallow: in Africa, as its migrated self,
following flying \omterm{prop:g3:sorting-living-things:groups}{insects}. The bees: in the hive, as the
winter cluster, warm around their queen.

\textbf{8.} Staying active. The magpie must find food
through the leanest months --- the very problem the swallow
escapes by leaving.

\textbf{9.} For example: \omterm{def:g6:life-through-seasons:buds}{buds} burst (March--April) as days
pass $11$--$13$ hours and mornings warm past
\qty{8}{\degreeCelsius}; \omterm{def:g1:plants-around-us:parts}{leaves} gild and drop
(October--November) as days shorten and mornings chill.
Tree events pair with condition changes frame after frame.

\textbf{10.} The patch did not die: it withdrew to storage
--- alive as \omterm{ex:g4:plants-without-seeds:bulbtuber}{bulbs} below the same spot. ``Gone'' always
deserves: gone \emph{where}, as \emph{what}?

\textbf{11.} Bare \omterm{def:g1:plants-around-us:tree}{branches} carrying \omterm{def:g6:life-through-seasons:buds}{buds}; a magpie likely
somewhere; below, \omterm{def:g1:plants-around-us:parts}{leaf} litter and bare daffodil ground
hiding \omterm{ex:g4:plants-without-seeds:bulbtuber}{bulbs}; poppy \omterm{prop:g3:flowers-fruits-seeds:transform}{seeds} in the path's earth. The certain
bets are the \omterm{def:g6:life-through-seasons:buds}{buds} and the bareness --- they follow from the
tree's \omterm{def:g6:life-through-seasons:trees}{deciduous} machinery and one year's proof; the magpie
is probable, not promised.

\textbf{12.} It shows warmth was the missing condition ---
\omterm{def:g6:exploring-our-environment:conditions}{light} indoors in February is no longer than outdoors, but
the water and room are warm, and that sufficed. Fair test:
two twigs from the same tree, both in water at the same
window, one in a warm room, one in a cold porch --- one
difference, warmth; if only the warm twig \omterm{def:g1:plants-around-us:parts}{leafs} out, the
verdict is nailed.
\end{solution}
