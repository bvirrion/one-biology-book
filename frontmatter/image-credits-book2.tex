The drawings in this book are original. The photographs are used
under their respective free licenses, with thanks to their authors:

\begin{itemize}
  \item \emph{Gregor Mendel} (meiosis chapter): photograph, 19th
        century, Wikimedia Commons, public domain.
  \item \emph{Alexander Fleming} (antibiotic resistance chapter):
        photograph, Imperial War Museums, 1943, via Wikimedia
        Commons, public domain.
  \item \emph{HIV budding from a lymphocyte} (adaptive immunity
        chapter): electron micrograph by C.~Goldsmith, Centers for
        Disease Control and Prevention, via Wikimedia Commons, public
        domain.
  \item \emph{Darwin's finches} (diversification chapter): engraving
        by John Gould, 1845, Wikimedia Commons, public domain.
  \item \emph{Sensory homunculus} (movement chapter): illustration
        from OpenStax \emph{Anatomy and Physiology}, via Wikimedia
        Commons, CC~BY~3.0.
  \item \emph{Charles Darwin} (phylogenetic trees chapter):
        photograph by Henry Maull and John Fox, c.~1854, Wikimedia
        Commons, public domain.
  \item \emph{Edward Jenner} (adaptive immunity chapter): oil
        painting, Wellcome Collection via Wikimedia Commons,
        CC~BY~4.0.
  \item \emph{Archaeopteryx fossil} (common ancestry chapter):
        photograph by James L.~Amos, Wikimedia Commons, CC0.
\end{itemize}

Full source links and license URLs for every photograph are listed
in \texttt{images/\allowbreak book2/\allowbreak CREDITS.md} and \texttt{images/\allowbreak book1/\allowbreak CREDITS.md}
in the book's repository.

\bigskip
Alongside the photographs and the original drawings, some fifty
figures spread across the volume's chapters are AI-generated
illustrations, produced with OpenAI image generation from prompts
written by the book's editors, and reviewed for biological accuracy
before inclusion. The full prompt for every generated image is
recorded in \texttt{images/\allowbreak book2/\allowbreak ai/\allowbreak PROMPTS.md} in the repository.
