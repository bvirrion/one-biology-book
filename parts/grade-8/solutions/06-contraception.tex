\section*{Chapter \ref{ch:g8:contraception} --- Contraception}

\begin{solution}{exo:g8:contraception:1}
\omterm{def:g8:sexual-reproduction:gametes}{Gametes} produced --- sperm deposited --- the swim to the
\omterm{def:g8:reproductive-systems:female}{oviduct} --- an \omterm{def:g4:animal-reproduction:sexual}{egg cell} released and met --- \omterm{def:g5:flower-to-fruit:steps}{fertilization}
--- implantation. \omterm{prop:g8:contraception:principle}{Contraception} is any deliberate
interruption of a link of that chain.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g8:contraception:2}
The \omterm{def:g8:contraception:condom}{condom} blocks the deposit link: semen never enters the
partner's body. The pill blocks the release link: no
\omterm{prop:g8:reproductive-systems:cycle}{ovulation}, so no \omterm{def:g4:animal-reproduction:sexual}{egg cell} to meet.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g8:contraception:3}
Protection against the infections transmitted by sex ---
no other method on the shelf offers any.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g8:contraception:4}
Chiefly by delaying an \omterm{prop:g8:reproductive-systems:cycle}{ovulation} that has not yet
happened, within days after the fact. It is not a standing
method --- a last-resort brake, no substitute for a method
used before.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g8:contraception:5}
The school nurse and family doctor: confidential answers
and prescriptions, minors included. Family-planning
centers: free, judgment-free advice and methods. The
pharmacist: the emergency pill and its clock.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g8:contraception:6}
\omterm{prop:g8:reproductive-systems:cycle}{Ovulation}'s date is not knowable in advance --- cycles
vary between people and months --- and sperm survive for
days, waiting. A calendar can neither pin the release nor
outwait the swimmers.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g8:contraception:7}
Meeting blocked: the \omterm{def:g8:contraception:condom}{condom} (deposit), the in-uterus
devices (meeting and implantation made to fail). Release
blocked: the pill, and the implants and patches --- the
same principle with better memory. After the fact: the
emergency pill, delaying release.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g8:contraception:8}
The pill guards the chain --- no \omterm{def:g4:animal-reproduction:sexual}{egg cell} released, so no
\omterm{def:g5:human-reproduction:pregnancy}{pregnancy}; the \omterm{def:g8:contraception:condom}{condom} guards the partners --- the one
barrier infections cannot cross. Two different dangers,
one method each.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g8:contraception:9}
In the suspended state of event 4 --- the state a
\omterm{def:g5:human-reproduction:pregnancy}{pregnancy}'s own \omterm{def:g8:puberty:hormones}{hormones} would impose --- but without any
\omterm{def:g5:human-reproduction:pregnancy}{pregnancy}: the \omterm{def:g8:reproductive-systems:female}{ovaries} release no \omterm{def:g4:animal-reproduction:sexual}{egg cell} while the
signal holds. Nothing released, nothing to fertilize,
nothing to implant.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g8:contraception:10}
The chain runs through both bodies --- one's \omterm{def:g8:sexual-reproduction:gametes}{gametes}, the
other's cycle and lodging --- and its consequences land on
both lives. A link either partner could block is both
partners' to discuss: method, memory and follow-through
shared, not delegated.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g8:contraception:11}
(a) Implant: link --- release, held closed hormonally;
reliability --- among the best, no memory demanded for
years; infections --- none, \omterm{def:g8:contraception:condom}{condom} partners it; asks --- a
doctor's fitting. A method on every count. (b)
Withdrawal: link --- none nameable, since sperm travel
before the finale; reliability --- poor exactly for that
reason; the evaluation ends at question 1: not a method.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g8:contraception:12}
The pill aims \cref{ch:g8:hormonal-communication}'s
system --- speaking \omterm{def:g8:puberty:hormones}{hormone} to the \omterm{def:g8:reproductive-systems:female}{ovaries};
the \omterm{def:g8:contraception:condom}{condom} aims
\cref{ch:g8:reproductive-systems}' map --- a barrier at
the deposit step; the in-uterus devices aim
\cref{ch:g8:from-egg-to-birth}'s first week --- meeting
and implantation refused. Each method is a chapter's
mechanism, deliberately interrupted.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{pb:g8:contraception:1}
\textbf{1.} The chain drawn left to right --- \omterm{def:g8:sexual-reproduction:gametes}{gametes},
deposit, swim, release-and-meeting, \omterm{def:g5:flower-to-fruit:steps}{fertilization},
implantation --- with barriers marked at deposit,
hormonal methods at release, and the device methods at
meeting/implantation.

\textbf{2.} It speaks the body's own \omterm{def:g8:puberty:hormones}{hormone} language to
the control chain of the cycle: a daily dose holds the
``suspended'' signal, and the \omterm{def:g8:reproductive-systems:female}{ovaries}, obedient to their
own system's messenger, release nothing.

\textbf{3.} Missed days let the signal lapse: the cycle's
machinery resumes and an \omterm{prop:g8:reproductive-systems:cycle}{ovulation} can slip through ---
the release link reopens, unannounced.

\textbf{4.} Because whatever guards the chain, only the
\omterm{def:g8:contraception:condom}{condom} guards against infections --- the second duty no
other method shares.

\textbf{5.} \omterm{prop:g8:reproductive-systems:cycle}{Ovulation} cannot be dated in advance ---
cycles vary --- and sperm outwait calendars by surviving
days. The app measures neither.

\textbf{6.} The implant or patch (release held closed,
months at a time) and the in-uterus device (meeting and
implantation refused, years at a time) --- both
memory-free by design.

\textbf{7.} The emergency pill; its clock --- the sooner
the better, within days; its chief mechanism --- delaying
an \omterm{prop:g8:reproductive-systems:cycle}{ovulation} not yet happened. Afterwards: a standing
method chosen properly --- the brake is not the brakes.

\textbf{8.} ``Whatever else you use: only the \omterm{def:g8:contraception:condom}{condom} also
protects you both from infections --- keep it in the
picture.''

\textbf{9.} Because a user who can name the link can
evaluate any method they will ever hear of --- and see
through every legend that names none. The center teaches
the map so the boxes make sense.

\textbf{10.} Daily memory: the pill. A fitting or
placement: implant, device. Only when needed: the \omterm{def:g8:contraception:condom}{condom}.
A clock and a pharmacy: the emergency pill.

\textbf{11.} Because the help map only works if it is
safe to use: confidentiality is what lets a
seventeen-year-old choose knowledge over legend ---
remove it, and the questions go back to the playground.

\textbf{12.} ``The center dispenses the mechanism's map
and the choice it makes possible --- the boxes only
implement it.''
\end{solution}
