\section*{Chapter \ref{ch:g6:matter-from-food} --- Matter From Food}

\begin{solution}{exo:g6:matter-from-food:1}
\omterm{def:g5:food-webs:roles}{Consumers} transform \omterm{def:g6:plants-are-producers:matter}{organic matter} they eat into their own
\omterm{def:g6:plants-are-producers:matter}{organic matter}. \omterm{def:g5:food-webs:roles}{Producers} make new \omterm{def:g6:plants-are-producers:matter}{organic matter} from
purely mineral supplies, with \omterm{def:g6:exploring-our-environment:conditions}{light}'s \omterm{prop:g3:balanced-diet:jobs}{energy}.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g6:matter-from-food:2}
\omterm{def:g4:journey-of-food:digestion}{Digestion} dismantles the grass into \omterm{def:g4:journey-of-food:digestion}{nutrients}
(\cref{ch:g4:journey-of-food}); the \omterm{prop:g4:journey-of-food:absorb}{blood} delivers them
everywhere (\cref{ch:g4:heart-and-blood}); the body's \omterm{def:g5:first-look-at-cells:cell}{cells}
reassemble them into cow-matter, dividing as the body grows
(\cref{ch:g5:first-look-at-cells}).
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g6:matter-from-food:3}
Growth (the calf's added kilograms); renewals (molted \omterm{def:g1:animals-around-us:covering}{fur},
new \omterm{def:g1:animals-around-us:covering}{feathers}, antlers); repairs (a mended \omterm{def:g3:bones-and-muscles:skeleton}{bone}); products
(\omterm{def:g2:animals-grow-up:born}{milk}, \omterm{def:g2:animals-grow-up:born}{eggs}, wool).
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g6:matter-from-food:4}
Never absorbed --- out as droppings; burned with the
breath's oxygen for movement and warmth --- out as exhaled
gas; and the remainder, built into growth and products.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g6:matter-from-food:5}
Because each day's \qty{25}{kg} of \omterm{def:g2:animals-grow-up:born}{milk} is \omterm{def:g6:plants-are-producers:matter}{organic matter}
she must rebuild from that day's feed; \omterm{def:g2:animals-grow-up:born}{milk} is production,
and production is rebuilt food --- no input, no output.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g6:matter-from-food:6}
Assembled \emph{in} the hen, from the \omterm{def:g4:journey-of-food:digestion}{nutrients} of her
grain --- but the \omterm{def:g6:plants-are-producers:matter}{organic matter} itself was first
manufactured before her, in the grain \omterm{def:g1:plants-around-us:plant}{plant}'s \omterm{def:g1:plants-around-us:parts}{leaves}. The
hen transformed; the \omterm{def:g1:plants-around-us:plant}{plant} produced.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g6:matter-from-food:7}
At each arrow, most of the eaten matter is burned for
living or lost as droppings, and only a modest fraction
becomes eater's body --- the only part the next floor can
eat. Each floor therefore has only a fraction of the
matter below it: the pyramid narrows by arithmetic.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g6:matter-from-food:8}
In: meat or kibble (and the occasional mouse). Built: its
growth as a kitten, the \omterm{def:g1:animals-around-us:covering}{fur} it renews at each molt, repairs
after scrapes. Out: droppings in the garden, and the burned
share powering every pounce, purr and warm nap.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g6:matter-from-food:9}
What differs is the source and the equipment: grass versus
rabbit, grinders-and-vast-gut versus seizers-and-short-gut.
What is identical is the trade itself: both dismantle eaten
\omterm{def:g6:plants-are-producers:matter}{organic matter} into \omterm{def:g4:journey-of-food:digestion}{nutrients} and rebuild it as their own
substance.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g6:matter-from-food:10}
Most left as the burned share --- powering a year of
running, thinking and staying at
\qty{37}{\degreeCelsius} --- and a part was never absorbed
and left as droppings. Only the small remainder, the
\qty{3}{kg}, became new body: the child's ledger balances
like the cow's.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g6:matter-from-food:11}
The snail's body extracted the \omterm{def:g1:animals-around-us:covering}{shell}'s mineral ingredients
from its food and surroundings, transported them, and laid
them down layer by layer in its own architecture. The
substance is mineral; the \emph{building} is the living
\omterm{def:g1:animals-around-us:animal}{animal}'s work --- a made thing, of mineral material.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g6:matter-from-food:12}
Routed through the pig, most of the potatoes' matter is
spent on the pig's own living --- burned for warmth and
movement, lost as droppings --- and only a fraction returns
as pork. Eaten directly, the potatoes skip the pig's
expenses. The difference went up the pig's ledger, not onto
the table.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{pb:g6:matter-from-food:1}
\textbf{1.} The pasture's grasses and the vegetable patch's
\omterm{def:g1:plants-around-us:plant}{plants} (and any \omterm{prop:g3:flowers-fruits-seeds:transform}{fruit} trees). Inputs: water and \omterm{prop:g4:what-plants-need:needs}{mineral
salts} from the soil, carbon dioxide from the air. \omterm{prop:g3:balanced-diet:jobs}{Energy}:
\omterm{def:g6:exploring-our-environment:conditions}{light}.

\textbf{2.} In the grass \omterm{def:g1:plants-around-us:plant}{plants}' own \omterm{def:g1:plants-around-us:parts}{leaves} --- every gram:
\omterm{def:g5:food-webs:roles}{producers} manufacture on site, by day.

\textbf{3.} Return what you borrow: the manure's rotting
restocks the patch soil's \omterm{prop:g4:what-plants-need:needs}{mineral salts}, replacing what
harvests carry off.

\textbf{4.} Because the cow is a \omterm{def:g5:food-webs:roles}{consumer}: her trade needs
\emph{organic} matter to dismantle and rebuild. \omterm{prop:g4:what-plants-need:needs}{Mineral
salts} and water are a \omterm{def:g5:food-webs:roles}{producer}'s inputs; she has no
machinery to build body from minerals --- that is exactly
the trade she lacks.

\textbf{5.} Part passes through unabsorbed --- the cowpats;
a large part is burned with breathed oxygen to power
walking, chewing and staying warm; the rest becomes \omterm{def:g2:animals-grow-up:born}{milk}
and cow (and the ledger's day balances).

\textbf{6.} In: grain. Built: \omterm{def:g2:animals-grow-up:born}{eggs}, her own upkeep, and ---
in molt --- a full new coat of \omterm{def:g1:animals-around-us:covering}{feathers}. Out: droppings and
the burned share. Molting, the ``built'' line is spent on
\omterm{def:g1:animals-around-us:covering}{feathers}; with the input unchanged, the \omterm{def:g2:animals-grow-up:born}{egg} entry must
shrink for a while.

\textbf{7.} Grass $\rightarrow$ hen (via grain, itself a
\omterm{def:g1:plants-around-us:plant}{plant} product) $\rightarrow$ chick $\rightarrow$ buzzard;
or pasture-side: grass $\rightarrow$ cow. Matter was
\emph{made} only at the green links (grass, grain \omterm{def:g1:plants-around-us:plant}{plants});
every \omterm{def:g1:animals-around-us:animal}{animal} link --- hen, chick, buzzard, cow --- only
transformed it.

\textbf{8.} Bread: wheat \omterm{def:g1:plants-around-us:plant}{plant}'s \omterm{def:g1:plants-around-us:parts}{leaves}. Boiled \omterm{def:g2:animals-grow-up:born}{egg}: hen
from grain --- the grain \omterm{def:g1:plants-around-us:plant}{plant}'s \omterm{def:g1:plants-around-us:parts}{leaves}. \omterm{def:g2:animals-grow-up:born}{Milk}: cow from
grass --- the grass \omterm{def:g1:plants-around-us:parts}{leaves}. Three trails, three \omterm{def:g1:plants-around-us:parts}{leaves},
all in the \omterm{def:g6:exploring-our-environment:conditions}{light}.

\textbf{9.} Each floor passes on only a fraction: of the
\qty{10000}{kg} of grass, a rabbit population builds only
its modest fraction as body; of that, foxes build only a
fraction again. Two floors of losses shrink a pasture's
worth of grass to a den's worth of foxes --- her estimate
is the pyramid's arithmetic.

\textbf{10.} Eaten directly, the patch's produce reaches
the family with no \omterm{def:g5:food-webs:roles}{consumer} floor in between: none of it
is burned in an \omterm{def:g1:animals-around-us:animal}{animal}'s living or lost in its droppings.
The pasture route pays the cow's full expenses first, so
each square metre feeds fewer people.

\textbf{11.} The grass stops producing, so the cow's input
fails: first the \omterm{def:g2:animals-grow-up:born}{milk} shrinks (production is rebuilt food),
then she burns her own fat reserves and loses weight ---
the ``built'' line runs negative. Sunshine is \omterm{prop:g3:balanced-diet:jobs}{energy}, not
substance: with no water there is nothing for the grass to
build with, so the pasture cannot be rescued from the sky.

\textbf{12.} \omterm{def:g5:food-webs:roles}{Producers}: the holding's \omterm{def:g1:plants-around-us:plant}{plants} make all its
\omterm{def:g6:plants-are-producers:matter}{organic matter} from mineral supplies and \omterm{def:g6:exploring-our-environment:conditions}{light}. \omterm{def:g5:food-webs:roles}{Consumers}:
cow, hens and family transform that matter, burning most
and building the rest. Missing: the \omterm{def:g5:food-webs:roles}{decomposers} --- who
must return the droppings, cowpats and autumn remains to
\omterm{def:g6:plants-are-producers:matter}{mineral matter}; they are the next chapter's subject.
\end{solution}
