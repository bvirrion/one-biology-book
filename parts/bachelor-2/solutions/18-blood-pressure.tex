\section*{Chapter \ref{ch:b2:blood-pressure} --- Regulation of Blood Pressure and Exercise}

\begin{solution}{exo:b2:blood-pressure:1}
$\bar P - P_{\text{ven}} = Q\,R = f\,V_s\,R$. \omterm{def:b2:heart:anatomy}{Heart} rate (the
\omterm{prop:b2:heart:conduction}{sinoatrial node}, under the vagus and sympathetic nerves); \omterm{prop:b2:heart:cycle}{stroke
volume} (the \omterm{def:b2:heart:anatomy}{ventricle}: its filling, set by the \omterm{def:b2:blood-circulation:vessels}{veins}, and its
contractility, set by the sympathetic nerves); resistance (the
arteriolar smooth muscle, under the sympathetic nerves, hormones and
local metabolites).
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:b2:blood-pressure:2}
Sensors: stretch receptors in the carotid sinuses and aortic arch.
Centre: the cardiovascular centres of the medulla. Effectors: the
vagus (rate) and the sympathetic nerves (rate, contractility,
arteriolar and venous tone). Negative feedback, about one second's
delay. Without the sensors the mean pressure over a day stays
normal but the minute-to-minute pressure swings wildly with every
posture and emotion.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:b2:blood-pressure:3}
Kidney (arteriolar cells) releases renin when its perfusion or
sodium delivery falls or its sympathetic nerves fire; renin cuts
angiotensinogen (liver) to angiotensin I; lung enzyme converts it to
angiotensin II, which constricts \omterm{def:b2:blood-circulation:vessels}{arterioles} and stimulates the
adrenal cortex to secrete aldosterone; aldosterone makes the kidney
retain sodium and water, raising the \omterm{def:b2:blood-circulation:blood}{blood} volume.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:b2:blood-pressure:4}
Metabolic dilation of the muscle's own \omterm{def:b2:blood-circulation:vessels}{arterioles} by its
metabolites; a fivefold \omterm{thm:b2:heart:starling}{cardiac output} driven by the sympathetic
nerves and the muscle pump; sympathetic constriction of the
\omterm{def:b2:blood-circulation:vessels}{arterioles} of the gut, kidneys and resting muscle, which give up
flow.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:b2:blood-pressure:9}
$35/5 = \qty{7}{mmHg}$ down: about \qty{88}{mmHg}. Restoring the
volume: reabsorption of interstitial fluid into the capillaries
(minutes to an hour); reduced urine under \omterm{prop:b2:blood-pressure:raas}{antidiuretic hormone} and

and \omterm{def:b2:heart:anatomy}{heart}.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:b2:blood-pressure:10}
Muscle at $R = 0.5$: $95/0.5 = \qty{190}{mL/s}$, \qty{11.4}{L/min};
with the other organs' \qty{4}{L/min} the \omterm{def:b2:heart:anatomy}{heart} would need
\qty{15.4}{L/min}. With $Q$ fixed at \qty{5}{L/min}, the total
resistance falls from 1.14 to $1/(1/1.14 - 1/5 + 1/0.5) =
\qty{0.37}{mmHg.s/mL}$ and the pressure to \qty{31}{mmHg} --- the
brain would faint. The body raises the output toward the first case
and constricts the other beds, so that the pressure holds and the
muscle gets its flow.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:b2:blood-pressure:11}
(a) The underperfused kidney read a low pressure, released renin,
and angiotensin and aldosterone raised the resistance and the
volume. (b) The \omterm{def:b2:blood-pressure:baroreflex}{baroreflex} buffers changes but resets to whatever
level persists, and cannot alter the kidney's retention of volume.
(c) The source of renin, and the organ demanding a higher pressure,
was gone. (d) Blocked the conversion to angiotensin II: lower
resistance, less aldosterone, a lower pressure.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:b2:blood-pressure:12}
The \omterm{def:b2:blood-pressure:baroreflex}{baroreflex} acts in a second with a gain of about four and a set
point that drifts to the prevailing pressure: it damps fluctuations
and cannot hold a level. The kidney acts over hours and days, with
in effect infinite gain --- it goes on excreting or retaining until
the pressure it is set to is reached --- and its set point is fixed
by its own physiology: it holds the level. A chronic rise therefore
means the kidney's set point has moved, and only drugs that act on
the kidney's loop or on the resistance it demands bring it down.
\end{solution}
