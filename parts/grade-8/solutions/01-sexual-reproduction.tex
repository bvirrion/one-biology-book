\omterm{prop:g8:sexual-reproduction:core}{zygote} is the single new \omterm{def:g5:first-look-at-cells:cell}{cell} their joining forms. \omterm{def:g1:animals-around-us:animal}{Animals}:

\begin{solution}{exo:g8:sexual-reproduction:2}

\begin{solution}{exo:g8:sexual-reproduction:5}
One parent yields exact copies --- sameness the shuffle can
never give; two parents yield new combinations every time
--- variety that copying can never give.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g8:sexual-reproduction:6}
Each sibling grew from a different pair of \omterm{def:g8:sexual-reproduction:gametes}{gametes}, and no
two \omterm{def:g8:sexual-reproduction:gametes}{gametes} carry quite the same inheritance: every
\omterm{def:g5:flower-to-fruit:steps}{fertilization} deals a fresh combination of the two parents'
contributions.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g8:sexual-reproduction:7}
Copying (grafts, \omterm{met:g4:plants-without-seeds:cutting}{cuttings}) banks a proven excellence ---
right when the present variety is exactly what is wanted;
its price is shared weakness. Shuffling (pips) explores new
combinations --- right when improvement or insurance is
wanted; its price is that most deals are unremarkable.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g8:sexual-reproduction:8}
\omterm{def:g8:sexual-reproduction:gametes}{Gametes}: \omterm{def:g2:animals-grow-up:born}{eggs} from the \omterm{def:g4:animal-reproduction:sexual}{female}, sperm from the \omterm{def:g4:animal-reproduction:sexual}{male}, shed
in the pond. Meeting: external \omterm{def:g5:flower-to-fruit:steps}{fertilization} in water, the

for. Season: spring --- May's chorus.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g8:sexual-reproduction:9}
The meeting entry collapses --- no \omterm{def:g5:flower-to-fruit:steps}{fertilization} at all
--- yet the lodging, numbers and season entries read
normally: plumed \omterm{prop:g3:flowers-fruits-seeds:transform}{seeds} by the thousand, in summer. The
oddity teaches that the dossier is a checklist, not an
assumption: fill it from evidence, and a ``\omterm{def:g2:from-seed-to-plant:seed}{seed}'' need not
certify a shuffle. (The clone-by-seed dandelion gets a
copying bet with a \omterm{def:g6:how-plants-spread:dispersal}{dispersal} service --- both worlds.)
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g8:sexual-reproduction:10}
Two centuries of \omterm{met:g4:plants-without-seeds:cutting}{cuttings} made the vineyard one shared
constitution --- excellence banked, weakness shared. The
pest that relishes that constitution finds every vine
equally to its taste: no varied individuals, no reserve,
the copying bet called in at the worst table. The rescue,
historically as in the exercise, is the other bet:
crossing in resistant stock --- the shuffle.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g8:sexual-reproduction:11}
From the core: a \omterm{def:g8:sexual-reproduction:gametes}{gamete} alone dies --- only \omterm{def:g5:flower-to-fruit:steps}{fertilization},
joining two, makes the \omterm{prop:g8:sexual-reproduction:core}{zygote} that lives and divides. From
the shuffle: each \omterm{def:g8:sexual-reproduction:gametes}{gamete} carries only one parent's half of
a combination that does not exist until the joining deals
it.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g8:sexual-reproduction:12}
The \omterm{ex:g4:plants-without-seeds:runner}{runners} are the short-range, sure service: exact
copies densely occupying the good ground next door ---
this year's proven \omterm{def:g1:plants-around-us:plant}{plant}, banked. The \omterm{prop:g3:flowers-fruits-seeds:transform}{seeds} are the
long-range, exploring service: shuffled combinations
scattered far, stocking the \omterm{def:g5:biodiversity:species}{species}' reserve for changed
\omterm{def:g6:exploring-our-environment:conditions}{conditions} elsewhere. Keeping both buys the present and
the future at once.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{pb:g8:sexual-reproduction:1}
\textbf{1.} The brush plays pollinator: carrying \omterm{def:g4:animal-reproduction:sexual}{male}
\omterm{def:g8:sexual-reproduction:gametes}{gametes} from A to B's \omterm{def:g3:flowers-fruits-seeds:parts}{pistils} --- the meeting step. The
bags exclude bees so no stray \omterm{def:g3:flowers-fruits-seeds:parts}{pollen} adds a third parent:
control of the cross --- the fair test's spirit at the
\omterm{def:g3:flowers-fruits-seeds:parts}{flower}.

\textbf{2.} \omterm{prop:g3:flowers-fruits-seeds:transform}{Seeds} --- each pip an \omterm{def:g5:human-reproduction:pregnancy}{embryo} from one of B's
\omterm{def:g4:animal-reproduction:sexual}{egg cells} fertilized by one of A's \omterm{def:g3:flowers-fruits-seeds:parts}{pollen} \omterm{def:g5:first-look-at-cells:cell}{cells}: A's and
B's inheritances combined.

\textbf{3.} Because every pip is a different deal of the
shuffle: different \omterm{def:g8:sexual-reproduction:gametes}{gametes} on both sides, so three hundred
different combinations of crispness, resistance and all
else.

\textbf{4.} The orchard's two bets
(\cref{ex:g8:sexual-reproduction:bets}): most \omterm{prop:g2:from-seed-to-plant:cycle}{seedlings}
unremarkable, some worse, a rare one better --- the
shuffle's known spread.

\textbf{5.} The combination is shuffled away again: the
winner's pips deal \emph{new} mixtures of its inheritance
with its pollen-parent's, and crisp-and-resistant scatters
into fragments. Sowing the winner un-wins it.

\textbf{6.} Grafting --- \omterm{def:g4:plants-without-seeds:vegetative}{vegetative reproduction}: one
parent, exact copies. It guarantees the prized
combination, tree after tree.

\textbf{7.} The shared-weakness risk of the copying bet:
one constitution orchard-wide --- the vineyard's story
waiting to happen.

\textbf{8.} A reserve of variety: other varieties
interplanted, and breeding lines maintained from \omterm{def:g2:from-seed-to-plant:seed}{seed} ---
so that when a new blight or climate calls, the shuffle
has stock to deal from. The species-level reserve, kept
deliberately at orchard scale.

\textbf{9.} Phase one (the cross and the three hundred
\omterm{prop:g2:from-seed-to-plant:cycle}{seedlings}): the shuffle --- it \emph{found} the
combination. Phase two (grafting the winner): the copy ---
it \emph{kept} the combination. Exploration, then
banking.

\textbf{10.} Differing entries: meeting (external, in
water, no brush needed --- the breeder just pairs the
parents), lodging (\omterm{def:g2:animals-grow-up:born}{eggs} in a nest, not \omterm{prop:g3:flowers-fruits-seeds:transform}{seeds}), care (the
father fans the clutch), season. Invariant: \omterm{def:g8:sexual-reproduction:gametes}{gametes} made,
\omterm{def:g5:flower-to-fruit:steps}{fertilization} joining two into a \omterm{prop:g8:sexual-reproduction:core}{zygote}, the \omterm{prop:g8:sexual-reproduction:core}{zygote}
dividing into a shuffled individual --- the core cannot
differ, or it would not be \omterm{def:g4:animal-reproduction:sexual}{sexual reproduction}.

\textbf{11.} The shuffle stocks the table --- every
generation deals combinations no one designed; the
breeder's choosing is just the reserve principle run on
purpose: where nature's changed \omterm{def:g6:exploring-our-environment:conditions}{conditions} pick the
suited, she picks the crisp and resistant. No variety
dealt, nothing to choose.

\textbf{12.} \omterm{def:g4:animal-reproduction:sexual}{Sexual reproduction} was needed to
\emph{create} the new combination; \omterm{def:g4:plants-without-seeds:vegetative}{vegetative
reproduction} to \emph{multiply it unchanged}.
\end{solution}
