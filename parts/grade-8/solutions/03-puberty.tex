\section*{Chapter \ref{ch:g8:puberty} --- Puberty}

\begin{solution}{exo:g8:puberty:1}
The stage in which a child's body becomes an adult's ---
reproductive organs maturing and beginning \omterm{def:g8:sexual-reproduction:gametes}{gamete}
production, adult form taken on. It is the biological core
of \omterm{def:g3:stages-of-life:stages}{adolescence}.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g8:puberty:2}
Girls: breasts develop, hips widen, first menstrual
periods (with body hair). Boys: voice deepens, \omterm{def:g1:my-body:joint}{shoulders}
broaden, sperm production begins (with facial hair). Both:
the growth burst, oilier \omterm{def:g1:the-five-senses:sense}{skin}, changed sweat, mood
weather.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g8:puberty:3}
In girls, the first menstrual periods --- the cycles
starting up; in boys, the testes beginning to produce
\omterm{def:g4:animal-reproduction:sexual}{sperm cells}.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g8:puberty:4}
A chemical messenger released into the \omterm{prop:g4:journey-of-food:absorb}{blood} by a
specialized organ, carried everywhere by the circulation,
and obeyed by the organs built to respond to it.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g8:puberty:5}
It begins at a control center at the \omterm{def:g5:brain-in-command:system}{brain}'s base, whose
\omterm{def:g8:puberty:hormones}{hormones} wake the reproductive organs; their \omterm{def:g8:puberty:hormones}{hormones}, in
turn, order the whole body's rebuild --- so it ends
everywhere the \omterm{prop:g4:journey-of-food:absorb}{blood} reaches.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g8:puberty:6}
Both are normal: the timetable is personal --- early and
late both healthy, the order of changes variable, and the
destination shared. Years apart at thirteen says almost
nothing about either of them.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g8:puberty:7}
Because the messenger travels in the \omterm{prop:g4:journey-of-food:absorb}{blood}, which visits
every organ anyway: one broadcast reaches voice box,
\omterm{def:g1:my-body:joint}{shoulders} and \omterm{def:g1:the-five-senses:sense}{skin} alike, no wiring needed. The organs
that respond are those built to hear that messenger ---
addressing by receiver, not by route.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g8:puberty:8}
Cause one: the \omterm{def:g8:puberty:puberty}{puberty} \omterm{def:g8:puberty:hormones}{hormones} themselves reach the
\omterm{def:g5:brain-in-command:system}{brain}. Cause two: the \omterm{def:g5:brain-in-command:system}{brain} is simultaneously rewiring
toward its adult form. Reassurance: it is weather ---
normal, explicable, and passing.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g8:puberty:9}
Building matter above all --- the burst builds \omterm{def:g3:bones-and-muscles:skeleton}{bone} and
\omterm{def:g3:bones-and-muscles:muscle}{muscle} --- with dairy's bone-builders and steady \omterm{prop:g3:balanced-diet:jobs}{energy}
from the starchy family: the crash-diet reasoning of
\cref{exo:g7:digestion-and-nutrients:11}, run forward.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g8:puberty:10}
A construction site is most vulnerable mid-build:
tobacco, alcohol and the rest strike organs --- the \omterm{def:g5:brain-in-command:system}{brain}
first --- whose wiring and tissues are still being laid.
Damage to a finished structure is repair; damage to a
half-built one is design change.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g8:puberty:11}
Trustworthy: the school nurse, the family doctor (trained,
confidential, unsurprisable), this book (built to be
reread and checked). Not: the viral video (it sells,
trading on uncertainty), playground legend (retold guesses
with no address for corrections).
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g8:puberty:12}
Reach: one messenger changes voice, \omterm{def:g1:the-five-senses:sense}{skin}, \omterm{def:g3:bones-and-muscles:skeleton}{skeleton} and
mood at once --- the \omterm{prop:g4:journey-of-food:absorb}{blood} delivers everywhere
(observation: the coordinated rebuild). Coordination: the
changes run on one schedule though no \omterm{def:g5:brain-in-command:system}{nerve} connects the
sites (observation: beard and voice arriving together).
Difference from \omterm{def:g5:brain-in-command:system}{nerves}: no wiring, no split-second speed
--- orders take months and act body-wide, where
\cref{prop:g5:brain-in-command:loop}'s messages take
milliseconds and one path (observation: \omterm{def:g8:puberty:puberty}{puberty}'s slow
years against the catch's fifth of a second).
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{pb:g8:puberty:1}
\textbf{1.} All four show the \omterm{def:g3:stages-of-life:stages}{adolescent} burst. L: burst
nearly complete, flattening toward adult height. M: burst
not yet begun --- still on childhood's steady climb. N:
mid-burst. O: an early burst, run nine to twelve, now
plateauing.

\textbf{2.} ``Your chart is exactly a normal chart before
the burst: the timetable is personal, late is as healthy
as early, and your steady climb says the burst is coming
--- charts like yours turn steep within a year or two,
and land at the same adult heights as everyone's.''

\textbf{3.} The plateau ends the \emph{height} burst ---
growth in stature. \omterm{def:g8:puberty:puberty}{Puberty}'s other work --- filling out,
maturing, the \omterm{def:g5:brain-in-command:system}{brain}'s renovation --- continues beyond it;
O is early, not finished.

\textbf{4.} Because height mixes inheritance and age ---
a tall pre-burst child can out-measure a short mid-burst
one. The slope shows the process: steepness says burst,
flattening says its end, regardless of the line's height.

\textbf{5.} The waking of the control center at the
\omterm{def:g5:brain-in-command:system}{brain}'s base; its \omterm{def:g8:puberty:hormones}{hormones} ordered the reproductive
organs awake, and their \omterm{def:g8:puberty:hormones}{hormones} --- broadcast by the
\omterm{prop:g4:journey-of-food:absorb}{blood} --- ordered the growth plates and the rest.

\textbf{6.} \omterm{prop:g1:healthy-habits:needs}{Sleep}: growth and the \omterm{def:g5:brain-in-command:system}{brain}'s renovation run
at night --- short nights starve both. Diet: the burst
is paid in matter --- building matter and the plate's
families fund it. (Movement and no-sabotage complete the
care list.)

\textbf{7.} The \omterm{def:g8:puberty:hormones}{hormones} reach N's \omterm{def:g5:brain-in-command:system}{brain}, and the \omterm{def:g5:brain-in-command:system}{brain}
is mid-rewiring --- chemistry plus construction, normal
and passing. Practical: the clock drifts late but school
does not; N must defend the hours by an earlier,
screen-light-free wind-down
(\cref{prop:g5:healthy-choices:screens}).

\textbf{8.} Because the consulting room is trained,
confidential and unsurprised --- questions the chart
cannot show still have a proper address, and legend and
the internet's corners are not it.

\textbf{9.} L: burst ending, normal --- guard \omterm{prop:g1:healthy-habits:needs}{sleep}
through the exam years. M: pre-burst, normal --- patience
and the plate. N: mid-burst, normal --- \omterm{prop:g1:healthy-habits:needs}{sleep} hours and
building matter. O: early burst complete, normal ---
movement for the filling-out years.

\textbf{10.} Because normal is a wide road, not a line:
four healthy timetables, years apart, all arriving.

\textbf{11.} Nothing: the machinery is intact and its
timetable personal --- there is no fault to fix, and no
lever worth pulling on a healthy chart; the honest
prescription is time (with the care list while it
passes).

\textbf{12.} ``\omterm{def:g8:puberty:puberty}{Puberty} is the body's hormone-ordered
rebuild from child to adult --- run by messengers in the
\omterm{prop:g4:journey-of-food:absorb}{blood}, on a personal timetable, and funded by \omterm{prop:g1:healthy-habits:needs}{sleep},
food and movement.''
\end{solution}
