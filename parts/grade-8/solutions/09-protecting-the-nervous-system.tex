\begin{solution}{exo:g8:protecting-the-nervous-system:12}
The gaps that adjust are why the machine can learn: use
strengthens crossings, and skill, memory and every

juggler's month. The same adjustability is the

wrong lesson. One property, both faces: protect the
adjustability, and it compounds into skills; hand it to
junction-foolers, and it compounds into traps.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{pb:g8:protecting-the-nervous-system:1}
\textbf{1.} Prediction: the rested volunteer catches
centimetres sooner, steadier across tries. Mechanism:
\omterm{prop:g1:healthy-habits:needs}{sleep} consolidated the day's junctions and cleared the
chemical wear --- the short-nighted loop weighs sluggishly
at every \omterm{def:g8:nervous-communication:synapse}{synapse}.

\textbf{2.} ``The meter shows the dose rate; the plugs cut
it. The \omterm{def:g1:the-five-senses:sense}{ear}'s sensory receivers die by accumulated dose
and never regrow --- hearing is capital, spent loudest at
concerts and full-volume earbuds, defended by lower,
shorter, plugged.''

\textbf{3.} Imitate: nicotine --- posing as a natural
messenger at the reward junctions. Block: the paralyzing
venom --- the \omterm{def:g3:bones-and-muscles:muscle}{muscle} junction's messenger refused. Force:
alcohol fits the diagram as the system-wide damper (its
arrow: muffling the junctions' conversation end to end).

\textbf{4.} The target: the same reward junctions, trained
by the same unpredictable-prize schedule --- the
notification demo is a dosing demonstration with \omterm{def:g6:exploring-our-environment:conditions}{light} and
sound for molecules.

\textbf{5.} At fourteen the junctions are still being
built and are at their most adjustable: the same dose
rewires deeper and sets faster than in a finished \omterm{def:g5:brain-in-command:system}{brain}
--- ``turned out fine'' was played on easier difficulty.

\textbf{6.} The first cigarette's price is all the
following ones: each dose begins the reward junctions'
\omterm{def:g4:adapted-to-their-world:adaptation}{adaptation}, and the entrance is exactly one decision wide
--- the exit, years of rewired protest. What is offered is
the trap's doorway, not a cigarette.

\textbf{7.} Television scheduled its rewards; the feed
deals them unpredictably, item by item --- and
unpredictable small prizes are the schedule that trains
junctions hardest, which is why the checking resists
stopping as programs never did.

\textbf{8.} That stopping alone is fighting rewired
junctions, and nobody has to: nurses and doctors treat
the wiring condition confidentially --- minors included
--- and \omterm{def:g4:adapted-to-their-world:adaptation}{re-adaptation} works. First step: one trusted
adult, today, silence being the only losing move.

\textbf{9.} ``A month of juggling strengthens junctions
until three balls fly themselves --- that is learning,
and it is what your \omterm{def:g8:nervous-communication:synapse}{synapses} are for. \omterm{prop:g5:healthy-choices:substances}{Addiction} is the
identical mechanism: doses strengthen the wrong loop
until it runs itself. The \omterm{def:g5:brain-in-command:system}{brain} cannot offer one without
risking the other --- adjustability is the whole gift.
So the question is never whether your junctions will be
trained, only by what.''

\textbf{10.} Because the moment of the offer is the worst
moment to start weighing --- pressure loads the very
junctions doing the deciding. A sentence composed calmly
in advance turns the refusal into a reflex: pre-weighted
routing, ready before it is needed.

\textbf{11.} A defensible order: \omterm{prop:g1:healthy-habits:needs}{sleep} first (it
maintains everything else's gains), then no
junction-foolers (the irreversible trap), then loudness
(irreplaceable capital), then screens' hours, then
helmets. First choice defended: every other item's
benefits are consolidated overnight --- \omterm{prop:g1:healthy-habits:needs}{sleep} is the
maintenance the rest depend on.

\textbf{12.} ``We are protecting the adjustable junctions
that will have learned everything you ever know --- and
fourteen sits mid-decade in their fastest, most
trainable, most trappable years.''
\end{solution}
