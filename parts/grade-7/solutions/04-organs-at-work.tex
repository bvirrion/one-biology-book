\section*{Chapter \ref{ch:g7:organs-at-work} --- Organs at Work}

\begin{solution}{exo:g7:organs-at-work:1}
More \omterm{def:g7:what-is-respiration:respiration}{oxygen} and more \omterm{prop:g7:organs-at-work:bill}{glucose} taken from the \omterm{prop:g4:journey-of-food:absorb}{blood}; more
\omterm{def:g7:what-is-respiration:respiration}{carbon dioxide} and more heat released.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g7:organs-at-work:2}
The \omterm{prop:g4:journey-of-food:absorb}{blood}'s ready \omterm{def:g4:journey-of-food:digestion}{sugar-nutrient} --- the everyday fuel of
the \omterm{def:g5:first-look-at-cells:cell}{cells}' burning. The \omterm{def:g3:bones-and-muscles:muscle}{muscle} takes it from the \omterm{prop:g4:journey-of-food:absorb}{blood}
flowing through it (stocked, further back, by \omterm{def:g4:journey-of-food:digestion}{digestion}'s
deliveries).
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g7:organs-at-work:3}
\omterm{prop:g4:journey-of-food:absorb}{Blood} entering the \omterm{def:g3:bones-and-muscles:muscle}{muscle} is compared with \omterm{prop:g4:journey-of-food:absorb}{blood} leaving
it. At rest the leaving \omterm{prop:g4:journey-of-food:absorb}{blood} has given up only a modest
share of \omterm{def:g7:what-is-respiration:respiration}{oxygen} and \omterm{prop:g7:organs-at-work:bill}{glucose}; in hard work it \omterm{def:g1:plants-around-us:parts}{leaves}
stripped of most deliverable \omterm{def:g7:what-is-respiration:respiration}{oxygen}, and much richer in
\omterm{def:g7:what-is-respiration:respiration}{carbon dioxide}. The difference is the \omterm{def:g3:bones-and-muscles:muscle}{muscle}'s bill.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g7:organs-at-work:4}
\omterm{def:g4:breathing:movements}{Breathing} deepens and quickens; the \omterm{def:g4:heart-and-blood:heart}{heart} beats faster and
harder; the \omterm{prop:g4:journey-of-food:absorb}{blood} is redirected --- \omterm{def:g4:heart-and-blood:vessels}{vessels} of working
\omterm{def:g3:bones-and-muscles:muscle}{muscles} and cooling \omterm{def:g1:the-five-senses:sense}{skin} widen while the resting \omterm{def:g4:journey-of-food:tract}{digestive
tract}'s narrow.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g7:organs-at-work:5}
Burning fuel always yields warmth, and working \omterm{def:g3:bones-and-muscles:muscle}{muscle} is
the body's furnace. Shivering is \omterm{def:g3:bones-and-muscles:muscle}{muscles} set to work
producing nothing but that warmth --- heating on demand.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g7:organs-at-work:6}
The gradual return of \omterm{prop:g4:heart-and-blood:pulse}{pulse} and \omterm{def:g7:what-is-respiration:respiration}{breathing} after effort
ends. The body is reloading \omterm{def:g7:what-is-respiration:respiration}{oxygen} stores, clearing the
effort's wastes, and shedding accumulated heat.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g7:organs-at-work:7}
Rest about $72$; plateau near $160$; \omterm{def:g7:organs-at-work:recovery}{recovery} roughly five
to six minutes on the graph. Training lowers the resting
value, lowers the plateau for the same effort, and
shortens the \omterm{def:g7:organs-at-work:recovery}{recovery}.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g7:organs-at-work:8}
Both are rerouting effects. After lunch the \omterm{def:g4:journey-of-food:tract}{digestive
tract} holds a large share of the \omterm{prop:g4:journey-of-food:absorb}{blood}, so the rest of the
body runs sluggish; sprinting then forces the two floors
to compete for deliveries --- the stitch and the old rule
against racing on a full \omterm{def:g4:journey-of-food:tract}{stomach}.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g7:organs-at-work:9}
Widened \omterm{def:g1:the-five-senses:sense}{skin} \omterm{def:g4:heart-and-blood:vessels}{vessels} are the dispatch of furnace heat to
the body's radiator surface: a delivery of heat outward,
solving the working \omterm{def:g3:bones-and-muscles:muscle}{muscles}' overheating problem. The
flush is the cooling system running, not failing.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g7:organs-at-work:10}
The \omterm{def:g4:heart-and-blood:heart}{heart} moves more \omterm{prop:g4:journey-of-food:absorb}{blood} per beat --- everyday sign, a
lower resting \omterm{prop:g4:heart-and-blood:pulse}{pulse}. The \omterm{def:g3:bones-and-muscles:muscle}{muscles} grow and store more fuel
--- the same hill feels easier. The supply network's fine
\omterm{def:g4:heart-and-blood:vessels}{vessels} multiply and \omterm{def:g7:what-is-respiration:respiration}{breathing} deepens --- speech stays
possible during effort, and \omterm{def:g7:organs-at-work:recovery}{recovery} shortens.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g7:organs-at-work:11}
The $68$-rest, three-minute machine is the trained one.
The resting \omterm{prop:g4:heart-and-blood:pulse}{pulse} certifies a \omterm{def:g4:heart-and-blood:heart}{heart} that moves more per
beat; the \omterm{def:g7:organs-at-work:recovery}{recovery} time certifies supply lines that settle
the effort's debts fast. Together they are the two ends of
\cref{prop:g7:organs-at-work:training}'s rebuild.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g7:organs-at-work:12}
In a sprint, the \omterm{def:g1:my-body:parts}{leg} \omterm{def:g3:bones-and-muscles:muscle}{muscles}' \omterm{def:g5:first-look-at-cells:cell}{cells} burn \omterm{prop:g7:organs-at-work:bill}{glucose} with
\omterm{def:g7:what-is-respiration:respiration}{oxygen} to power contraction --- \omterm{def:g7:what-is-respiration:respiration}{respiration}'s purpose ---
and their bill (more fuel, more \omterm{def:g7:what-is-respiration:respiration}{oxygen} in; more \omterm{def:g7:what-is-respiration:respiration}{carbon
dioxide}, heat out) must be paid at once. The \omterm{prop:g4:journey-of-food:absorb}{blood} is the
paying service: loaded at \omterm{def:g7:how-animals-breathe:four}{lungs} and intestine, pumped
faster by the racing \omterm{def:g4:heart-and-blood:heart}{heart}, rerouted to the \omterm{def:g1:my-body:parts}{legs}, it
delivers the fuel and \omterm{def:g7:what-is-respiration:respiration}{oxygen} and carts off the \omterm{def:g7:what-is-respiration:respiration}{carbon
dioxide} --- one sprint, three chapters, a single account.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{pb:g7:organs-at-work:1}
\textbf{1.} Its \omterm{def:g7:what-is-respiration:respiration}{oxygen} and \omterm{prop:g7:organs-at-work:bill}{glucose} draw from the passing
\omterm{prop:g4:journey-of-food:absorb}{blood} multiplies; its \omterm{def:g7:what-is-respiration:respiration}{carbon dioxide} output and heat
output rise in step --- the bill of hard work.

\textbf{2.} Because they are two stages of one supply
line: \omterm{def:g7:what-is-respiration:respiration}{breathing} reloads the \omterm{prop:g4:journey-of-food:absorb}{blood} at the \omterm{def:g7:how-animals-breathe:four}{lungs}, the \omterm{def:g4:heart-and-blood:heart}{heart}
moves the reloaded \omterm{prop:g4:journey-of-food:absorb}{blood} to the \omterm{def:g3:bones-and-muscles:muscle}{muscles}. Demand raised at
one end raises the pace of both.

\textbf{3.} Working \omterm{def:g3:bones-and-muscles:muscle}{muscles} are furnaces; their heat must
leave. The \omterm{prop:g4:journey-of-food:absorb}{blood} carries it to the \omterm{def:g1:the-five-senses:sense}{skin}, whose widened
\omterm{def:g4:heart-and-blood:vessels}{vessels} flush and radiate --- furnace inside, radiator at
the surface.

\textbf{4.} Because debts remain: \omterm{def:g7:what-is-respiration:respiration}{oxygen} stores to
reload, wastes to clear, heat to shed. The \omterm{prop:g4:heart-and-blood:pulse}{pulse} falls
only as the accounts settle --- that falling is \omterm{def:g7:organs-at-work:recovery}{recovery}.

\textbf{5.} Rest $84 \to 72$: a \omterm{def:g4:heart-and-blood:heart}{heart} rebuilt to move
more per beat. Effort $168 \to 150$: the same step-ups
now cost less --- stronger \omterm{def:g3:bones-and-muscles:muscle}{muscles}, richer fine-vessel
supply. \omterm{def:g7:organs-at-work:recovery}{Recovery} $9 \to 5$ minutes: the whole chain
settles its debts faster. The term's exercise did the
rebuilding.

\textbf{6.} Either B began the term already trained ---
numbers near their trained floor move little --- or B
skipped the term's exercise and merely kept a stable
untrained machine. Deciding fact: B's activity outside
class (sport elsewhere would confirm the first reading;
none, the second --- and B's good week-1 numbers lean
that way already).

\textbf{7.} The rerouting rule: after a meal the
\omterm{def:g4:journey-of-food:tract}{digestive tract} claims the \omterm{prop:g4:journey-of-food:absorb}{blood}, so resting \omterm{prop:g4:heart-and-blood:pulse}{pulse} reads
high and effort tolerance low. Protocol: measure before
lunch, or a good two hours after --- always the same
slot.

\textbf{8.} The fair test: only measurements taken the
same way can be compared, week against week and student
against student.

\textbf{9.} ``Twelve weeks raised A's ceiling: the same
effort now costs $150$ beats instead of $168$, from a
resting \omterm{prop:g4:heart-and-blood:pulse}{pulse} eight beats lower. And A's machine settles
its debts in five minutes where September needed nine ---
\omterm{def:g7:organs-at-work:recovery}{recovery} is the clearest gain of all.''

\textbf{10.} Usually the opposite of worrying: a trained
\omterm{def:g4:heart-and-blood:heart}{heart} moves more \omterm{prop:g4:journey-of-food:absorb}{blood} per beat and so idles low --- the
cyclist's calm $50$ doing an untrained $75$'s work. (Any
concern belongs to a doctor, but low-and-fit is the
common story.)

\textbf{11.} \omterm{def:g7:organs-at-work:recovery}{Recovery} time measures the whole chain ---
\omterm{def:g4:heart-and-blood:heart}{heart}, \omterm{def:g7:how-animals-breathe:four}{lungs}, \omterm{def:g4:heart-and-blood:vessels}{vessels}, \omterm{def:g3:bones-and-muscles:muscle}{muscles} --- settling real debts,
and it improves steadily and visibly with training;
effort \omterm{prop:g4:heart-and-blood:pulse}{pulse} depends more on the day's exact effort and
mood. As a single tracked number, \omterm{def:g7:organs-at-work:recovery}{recovery} is the
honester summary.

\textbf{12.} Its own use: effort, repeated gently and
regularly, is what rebuilt the machine that performs it.
\end{solution}
