\begin{solution}{exo:g9:our-responsibility:11}

and \omterm{def:g6:exploring-our-environment:conditions}{lights}, or data? Test: was there a fair trial ---

miracle? Five steps, and such posts rarely survive the
first two.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{pb:g9:our-responsibility:1}
\textbf{1.} Droplets and hands --- hence cover-the-cough,
wash, air the rooms, stay home ill. Staff first because
they connect the building's most fragile: their training
pools exactly where the untrainable are gathered.

\textbf{2.} The register stocks matched \omterm{prop:g4:journey-of-food:absorb}{blood} for the
day of accidents and surgeries; the ledger stocks trained
memories. Drawers: anyone --- the register's match found
by group, the pool's shield covering the young, old and
ill.

\textbf{3.} For example: ``A ward once halved its
mothers' deaths with nothing but washed hands: the
`fuss' interrupts couriers no \omterm{def:g1:the-five-senses:sense}{eye} can see, and the
century and a half since Semmelweis has only confirmed
that the cheapest medicine in existence runs from a soap
dish.''

\textbf{4.} The antibiotic commons, by ragged selection:
each unfinished course spares and promotes the resistant
minority, spending the drug's power against everyone's
future \omterm{def:g9:microbes-and-infection:stages}{infections}.

\textbf{5.} The spray would take pollinators and
pest-eaters with the pests: \omterm{prop:g3:flowers-fruits-seeds:transform}{fruit} set falls with the
bees, and the survivors' next outbreak meets no
ladybugs, no tits --- the free services stop, and the
bill returns as rented hives and more spray.

\textbf{6.} The pea-green bloom respires all night in
warm still water that holds little \omterm{def:g7:what-is-respiration:respiration}{oxygen}: the dawn
minimum hit bottom
(\cref{ex:g7:oxygen-in-the-water:curve}). Prescribe:
night-running fountain, thin the mud and overgrowth ---
and mind the \omterm{def:g7:digestion-and-nutrients:families}{nutrient} inflows that fed the bloom.

\textbf{7.} \omterm{def:g2:where-animals-live:habitat}{Habitat}: the marsh is breeding ground ---
no \omterm{def:g2:where-animals-live:habitat}{habitat}, no inhabitants. Web: its \omterm{def:g5:biodiversity:species}{species} pollinate,
clean and feed the surroundings' chains. Reserve: its
varied \omterm{def:g8:reproduction-and-environment:population}{populations} bank the \omterm{def:g9:genes-and-dna:gene}{alleles} hard times will
call. (Storks return where meadows do.)

\textbf{8.} The unpaid ledger: flood water stored and
released slowly, water filtered, pollinators housed,
young \omterm{prop:g3:sorting-living-things:groups}{fish} nursed --- services billed only when the
marsh is drained and the town buys them from engineers.

\textbf{9.} Open answers; a defensible pair: best ---
the reserve (capital banked under all three lasting-use
rules); worst --- unfinished courses or the spray
proposal (commons spent, workers evicted), judged by
take-no-faster, keep-the-workers, return-what-you-borrow.

\textbf{10.} Observed: dead \omterm{prop:g3:sorting-living-things:groups}{fish} at dawn, green water,
readings low. Fair test: dawn against afternoon probes;
this pond against a clear one. Mechanism named: night
\omterm{def:g7:what-is-respiration:respiration}{respiration} of the bloom under a lowered warm-water
ceiling. Witnesses: probe data, the census's tolerant
survivors, the timing --- all agreeing. Respect: the
prescription manages the pond's living machinery rather
than dosing symptoms.

\textbf{11.} For example: ``Our flu wave ended where
hands were washed and sick days taken --- health is a
shared account. The marsh we saved is capital, not
scenery, and it will pay in floods held and harvests
pollinated. The year's loudest claims fared worst under
one question: name the mechanism. Keep the method: it
is the town's cheapest infrastructure.''

\textbf{12.} Open answer --- one honest possibility:
``The snail and you were built by the same four
letters: look after the whole family.''
\end{solution}
